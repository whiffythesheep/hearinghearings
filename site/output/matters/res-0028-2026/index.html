In brief

This resolution calls on the U.S. Congress to establish a firm date for ending the Financial Oversight and Management Board for Puerto Rico, created under a 2016 federal law to restructure the island's debt. It notes that in August 2025 President Trump removed six of the board's seven members without naming replacements, and that a federal judge later blocked the removal of three of them. The Council argues Puerto Rico should be self-governing rather than overseen by this board.
