Official title

Resolution calling on the United States Congress to establish a date certain for ending the Financial Oversight and Management Board for Puerto Rico

Summary

This resolution calls on the United States Congress to establish a definite date for ending the Financial Oversight and Management Board for Puerto Rico, which was created in 2016 under PROMESA. It notes that in August 2025 President Trump removed six of the board's seven appointees without naming replacements, and that a federal judge later blocked some of those removals. The resolution argues Puerto Rico should be self-governing by its own democratically elected officials.
