Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to prohibiting the sale and rental of class three bicycles with electric assist

Summary

This bill would prohibit businesses from selling or renting class three e-bikes, which are electric bicycles that can go up to 25 miles per hour without pedaling. A first violation would carry no penalty, but each subsequent violation within two years could bring a civil fine of up to $2,000. The law would take effect 180 days after being enacted.
