Law requiring yearly report on park accessibility

· Sponsor: Shahana K. Hanif
+ 4 co-sponsors Shekar Krishnan, Linda Lee, Farah N. Louis, Gale A. Brewer
· View on Legistar ↗

Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to an annual report on compliance with the Americans with disabilities act standards for accessible design by the department of parks and recreation, and to repeal and replace section 18-143 of the administrative code of the city of New York

Summary

This bill would require the Parks Department to annually assess park entrances, bathrooms, playgrounds, beaches and pools for compliance with Americans with Disabilities Act design standards. By December 31 each year, Parks would report to the mayor and Council on each facility's compliance status, needed fixes, timelines and completed work, and post a map of all assessed facilities online. The bill repeals and replaces the current accessibility reporting law, section 18-143 of the Administrative Code.
