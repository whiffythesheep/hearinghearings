Int 0178-2026 (Passed Council: awaiting the Mayor)

Law to create a city office helping building-to-housing conversions

· Sponsor: Harvey D. Epstein
+ 14 co-sponsors Farah N. Louis, Christopher Marte, Shahana K. Hanif, Tiffany L. Cabán, Virginia Maloney, Oswald J. Feliz, Selvena N. Brooks-Powers, Ty Hankerson, Shekar Krishnan, Carl Wilson, Shirley Aldebol, Chris Banks, Kevin C. Riley, Frank Morano
· View on Legistar ↗

Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to providing support for the conversion of buildings to a residential use

Summary

This bill would require the Department of Housing Preservation and Development, Department of Buildings, Department of City Planning, Board of Standards and Appeals and Landmarks Preservation Commission to provide coordinated technical assistance to owners and developers converting non-residential buildings to housing. It would create an office of conversion assistance, led by a director, to serve as a single point of contact, maintain a public website with guidance and data, and liaise with state and federal agencies on permits and funding programs. The office would issue a public report every three years on conversions and progress citywide.
