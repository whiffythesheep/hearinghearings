Int 1025-2026 (In committee: awaiting a hearing)

Law to require scanning and 15-minute delay for police radio access

· Sponsor: Gale A. Brewer · View on Legistar ↗

Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to the use of time-delays and scanning technologies for encrypted police radio communications

Summary

This bill would require the NYPD to let credentialed journalists access encrypted police radio channels no more than 15 minutes after transmission. It also requires the broadcast system to allow users to scan and cycle through available channels to locate transmissions. The law would take effect immediately upon enactment.
