Int 1063-2026 (In committee: heard, awaiting a vote)

Law to allow small electric motors on pedicabs

· Sponsor: Carl Wilson
+ 1 co-sponsor Gale A. Brewer
· View on Legistar ↗

Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to pedicabs with electric assist

Summary

This bill would let pedicabs use an electric motor under 750 watts that assists a driver's pedaling but stops helping once the pedicab reaches 15 miles per hour, or another speed set by the Department of Transportation. It also lets such pedicabs be up to 55 inches wide. The change would legalize electric assist that city officials say is already used by most pedicabs despite a current ban on motors.
