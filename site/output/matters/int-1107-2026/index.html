Int 1107-2026 (In committee: heard, awaiting a vote)

Law to require guidance on service animal access rights

· Sponsor: Shahana K. Hanif
+ 4 co-sponsors Kevin C. Riley, Farah N. Louis, Gale A. Brewer, Frank Morano
· View on Legistar ↗

Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to guidance and public education regarding service animal access in places of public accommodation

Summary

This bill would require the Department of Health and Mental Hygiene, working with the Commission on Human Rights and the Mayor's Office for People with Disabilities, to create guidance and a public education campaign about service animal access at businesses and other public places. It would require pamphlets, model signage and online materials explaining what counts as a service animal, what businesses must allow, and how to file a complaint if denied access. The materials must be reviewed and updated at least every three years, starting within one year of the law taking effect.
