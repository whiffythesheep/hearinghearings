Int 0804-2026 (Law: Local Law 151 of 2026)

Law to limit bright outdoor lights in residential yards

· Enacted October 10, 2026 · Sponsor: Phil Wong
+ 4 co-sponsors Farah N. Louis, Harvey D. Epstein, Gale A. Brewer, Chris Banks
· View on Legistar ↗

Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to the maximum allowable illuminance of residential outdoor lighting fixtures

Summary

This bill would ban outdoor lighting brighter than 3,000 lumens in residential districts unless the fixture is fully shielded to prevent light from spilling onto neighboring properties. It exempts government-operated lighting and lighting for parking lots or sidewalk sheds. Violators would face a $50 civil penalty, and the law would take effect 180 days after enactment.
