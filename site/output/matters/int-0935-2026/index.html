Int 0935-2026 (Law: Local Law 146 of 2026)

Law to require police reports on gunfire incidents

· Enacted September 12, 2026 · Sponsor: Oswald J. Feliz
+ 7 co-sponsors Joann Ariola, Shanel Thomas-Henry, Farah N. Louis, Lynn C. Schulman, Eric Dinowitz, Phil Wong, Frank Morano
· View on Legistar ↗

Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to quarterly reporting on confirmed firearm discharges

Summary

This bill would require the NYPD to report quarterly on confirmed firearm discharges in the city, including the date, time and location of each incident. The report would not cover discharges by NYPD officers acting within their duties. The law would take effect one year after being enacted.
