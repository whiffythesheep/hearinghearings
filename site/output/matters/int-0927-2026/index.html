Int 0927-2026 (Passed Council: awaiting the Mayor)

Law to rename part of Shore Front Parkway in Queens

· Sponsor: Selvena N. Brooks-Powers
+ 2 co-sponsors Lynn C. Schulman, Harvey D. Epstein
· View on Legistar ↗

Official title

A Local Law in relation to renaming one thoroughfare and public place in the Borough of Queens, Edgemere Avenue, and to amend the official map of the city of New York accordingly

Summary

This bill would rename Shore Front Parkway, between Beach 32nd Street and Beach 56th Place in Queens, to Edgemere Avenue. The City's official map would be amended to reflect the new name. The law would take effect immediately upon passage.
