Resolution setting how property tax burden splits across classes

· Sponsor: Linda Lee · View on Legistar ↗

Official title

Resolution to amend and restate the resolution computing and certifying base percentage, current percentage, and current base proportion of each class of real property for Fiscal 2027 to the State Board of Real Property Tax Services pursuant to Section 1803-a of the Real Property Tax Law

Summary

This resolution updates the formula the City Council certifies to the state for dividing the Fiscal 2027 property tax levy among the four classes of real property, following a 2026 state law change. It applies a 0.3 percent cap on increases for Classes 1 and 2, shifting the excess share onto Classes 3 and 4. The measure replaces a version adopted on June 30, 2026 and is tied to a companion resolution the Council will also vote on November 12, 2026.
