Int 0161-2026 (In committee: heard, awaiting a vote)

Law to track city jobs affected by AI tools

· Sponsor: Carmen N. De La Rosa
+ 7 co-sponsors Jennifer Gutiérrez, Farah N. Louis, Lynn C. Schulman, Virginia Maloney, Tiffany L. Cabán, Mercedes Narcisse, Selvena N. Brooks-Powers
· View on Legistar ↗

Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to requiring reporting on the impact of algorithmic tools on city employees and changes in employment responsibilities due to algorithmic tools

Summary

This bill would require city agencies to report how algorithmic tools affect their employees, including the number of positions eliminated, hours reduced, salaries changed and staff required to undergo new training because of such tools. The information would be added to the city's existing annual algorithmic tools compliance report. The law would take effect immediately upon passage.
