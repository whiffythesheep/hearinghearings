Official title

Oversight - Study Abroad Opportunities at CUNY.

Summary

The Council's Committee on Higher Education will hold an oversight hearing on study abroad opportunities offered across CUNY's 26 colleges, examining program advertising, affordability, and enrollment by student background. The hearing will also look at CUNY's summer and fall 2026 programs and its plans for sustaining and expanding study abroad offerings. Witnesses include CUNY administration, faculty and student senates, and the Professional Staff Congress.
