Int 0894-2026 (Law: Local Law 143 of 2026)

Law to allow winter enclosures for sidewalk cafes

· Enacted September 12, 2026 · Sponsor: Gale A. Brewer
+ 3 co-sponsors Farah N. Louis, Justin E. Sanchez, Crystal Hudson
· View on Legistar ↗

Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to vertical winterization coverings for sidewalk cafes

Summary

This bill would let licensed sidewalk cafe operators install removable vertical screenings and overhead coverings each year from October 1 through March 31. Currently sidewalk cafes must remain open-air with only removable tables, chairs and decorative items. The law would take effect 120 days after being enacted.
