Official title

A Local Law to amend the New York city charter and the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to amending and eliminating certain reporting requirements, to amend section 5 of such charter, relating to an annual statement to the council, to repeal section 17 of such charter, relating to a strategic policy statement, to repeal section 20-e of such charter, relating to a committee on city healthcare services, to amend subdivision g of section 20-m of such charter, relating to a report identifying critical gaps in mental health care, to amend subdivision c of section 2301 of such charter, relating to membership of the New York city taxi and limousine commission, to amend subdivision a of section 2302 of such charter, relating to an annual report by the New York city taxi and limousine commission, to amend paragraph 6 of subdivision b of section 2903 of such charter, relating to a report on the condition of all bridges and tunnels operated and maintained by the department...

Summary

This bill would repeal or amend 24 Charter and Administrative Code provisions that require city agencies to produce reports or studies, such as those on taxi commission complaints, bridge conditions, domestic violence, homeless deaths and pesticide use. Many requirements are repealed because their deadlines have passed or they duplicate other reporting, while others get updated due dates. The law would take effect immediately.
