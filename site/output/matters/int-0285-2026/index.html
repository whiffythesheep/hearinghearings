Int 0285-2026 (In committee: heard, awaiting a vote)

Law to expand the Utility Advocate's outreach duties

· Sponsor: Farah N. Louis
+ 3 co-sponsors Chris Banks, Althea V. Stevens, Frank Morano
· View on Legistar ↗

Official title

A Local Law to amend the New York city charter and the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to expanding the outreach and advocacy duties of the office of the utility advocate

Summary

This bill would expand the Office of the Utility Advocate's responsibilities to include hosting at least four public trainings a year on utility billing and affordability, educating customers about their rights, and helping them connect with financial assistance programs. It would also require the office to publicize upcoming Public Service Commission rate cases and monitor policy issues affecting utility affordability. The law would take effect 60 days after being enacted.
