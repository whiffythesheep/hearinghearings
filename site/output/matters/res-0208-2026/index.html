Resolution urging Albany to require anti-bias training for medical students

· View on Legistar ↗

Official title

Resolution calling upon the New York State legislature to pass, and the Governor to sign, S.319/A.5625, which mandates all medical students in New York State to receive anti-bias training

Summary

This resolution calls on the New York State Legislature to pass, and the Governor to sign, S.319/A.5625, which would require anti-bias training for all medical students, medical residents and physician assistant students in New York State. The bill would direct the State Department of Health to work with the State Board of Medicine and the Division of Human Rights to set training guidelines and report annually on their implementation. The Council cites studies on racial and other disparities in healthcare as the basis for the request.
