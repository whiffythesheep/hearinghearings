Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to establishing organic waste composting facilities in each borough

Summary

This bill would require the Department of Sanitation to build one or more organic waste composting facilities in each borough, each able to process at least 180,000 wet tons of organic waste a year. Sites would need community engagement, environmental justice review, consultation with a facility siting task force and use of minority- and women-owned business contractors. Staten Island and Queens facilities would be required by January 1, 2026, Brooklyn and the Bronx by March 1, 2027, and Manhattan by May 1, 2027.
