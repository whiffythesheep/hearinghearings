Int 1128-2026 (In committee: heard, awaiting a vote)

Law to set default deadlines for agency reports to Council

· Sponsor: Nantasha M. Williams
+ 10 co-sponsors Crystal Hudson, Linda Lee, Farah N. Louis, Virginia Maloney, Althea V. Stevens, Kevin C. Riley, Amanda C. Farías, Shanel Thomas-Henry, Lynn C. Schulman, Frank Morano
· View on Legistar ↗

Official title

A Local Law to amend the New York city charter, in relation to the due dates of agency reports to the council

Summary

This bill would create a standard default due date for agency reports to the City Council when a law does not already specify one. Reports covering less than a year would be due at the end of the month after the reporting period ends, while reports covering a year or more would be due at the end of the second month after. It also standardizes how terms like month, quarter and fiscal year are defined in reporting laws.
