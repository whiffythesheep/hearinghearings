LU 0160-2026 (Passed Council: adopted)

Tax break for affordable housing at 160-159 Realty, Manhattan

· Sponsor: Linda Lee · Decided by Res 0745-2026 ↗ · View on Legistar ↗

Official title

160-159 Realty LLC.PLP.FY27: Block 2109, Lot 17; Block 2118, Lot 11, Manhattan, Community District 12, Council District 10.

Summary

The resolution approves a property tax exemption for a housing project at Block 2109, Lot 17 and Block 2118, Lot 11 in Manhattan, owned by 160/159 Realty LLC in partnership with a housing development fund company, under Section 577 of the Private Housing Finance Law. The exemption can last up to 40 years from its effective date and requires the property to remain in compliance with a regulatory agreement with the city's Department of Housing Preservation and Development. The Council's Finance Committee considered this measure on November 19, 2026.
