Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to abatement of taxation for alterations and improvements to certain multiple dwellings

Summary

This bill would extend the J-51 property tax abatement to alterations and improvements completed between June 30, 2026 and June 30, 2036. Eligible buildings include co-ops and condos with average assessed value under $60,000 per unit, buildings where more than half the units are affordable, buildings run by limited-profit housing companies, and buildings receiving substantial government assistance. Owners could recover up to 100% of certified costs over 20 years, subject to tenant protections and HPD reporting requirements.
