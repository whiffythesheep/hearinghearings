Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to the third party transfer program, and to repeal sections 11-425, 11-426, and 11-427 of such code, relating to agreements for payment of delinquent taxes and charges in installments

Summary

This bill would overhaul the City's Third Party Transfer program, which foreclosures on distressed, tax-delinquent buildings and transfers them to third parties for rehabilitation. It broadens the definition of distressed property, adds new notice requirements for owners and residents, and creates new ways for owners to avoid losing their property. It also repeals existing sections on installment payment agreements for delinquent taxes and charges.
