Resolution urging Albany to help foster agencies afford insurance

· Sponsor: Gale A. Brewer

· View on Legistar ↗

Official title

Resolution calling on the New York State Assembly to pass, and for the Governor to sign, A.9646A, whose companion bill S.9113A, has already been passed by the New York State Senate, to establish an insurance risk pool ensuring foster care agencies can obtain the required liability insurance to provide assistance with rapidly rising insurance costs, and, to update the State reimbursement rates to better reflect current insurance expenses, and to create the voluntary foster care agency insurance bridge fund

Summary

This resolution calls on the state Assembly to pass, and the Governor to sign, A.9646A to create an insurance risk pool helping foster care agencies obtain required liability insurance. It would also update state reimbursement rates to reflect rising insurance costs and create a voluntary foster care agency insurance bridge fund. Supporters say rising insurance costs threaten care continuity for more than 10,000 youth in foster care statewide.
