Int 0450-2026

Law to limit teen social media use to one hour a day

In committee · Sponsors: Althea V. Stevens, Kevin C. Riley, Nantasha M. Williams, Chris Banks, Selvena N. Brooks-Powers, Farah N. Louis, Lynn C. Schulman, Elsie Encarnación, Kamillah Hanks, Gale A. Brewer, Shirley Aldebol · View on Legistar ↗

In brief

This bill would require social media companies to cap use by anyone under 17 at one hour per day unless a parent or guardian waives the limit in writing. It would also ban targeted advertising and content recommendations aimed at youth. Companies could face a $5,000 fine per violation, and people harmed could sue for damages plus at least $5,000 per incident.
