Int 0450-2026 (In committee: heard, awaiting a vote)

Law to limit teen social media use to one hour a day

· Sponsor: Althea V. Stevens
+ 10 co-sponsors Kevin C. Riley, Nantasha M. Williams, Chris Banks, Selvena N. Brooks-Powers, Farah N. Louis, Lynn C. Schulman, Elsie Encarnación, Kamillah Hanks, Gale A. Brewer, Shirley Aldebol
· View on Legistar ↗

Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to restricting social media usage for youth

Summary

This bill would require social media companies to cap use by anyone under 17 at one hour per day unless a parent or guardian waives the limit in writing. It would also ban targeted advertising and content recommendations aimed at youth. Companies could face a $5,000 fine per violation, and people harmed could sue for damages plus at least $5,000 per incident.
