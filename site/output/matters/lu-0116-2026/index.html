LU 0116-2026 (Passed Council: adopted)

Zoning text change for 200 Kent Avenue, Brooklyn

· Sponsor: Kevin C. Riley · Decided by Res 0642-2026 ↗ · View on Legistar ↗

Official title

Application number N 260150 ZRK (200 Kent Avenue Rezoning) submitted by 206 Kent LLC and Kent Investor LLC, pursuant to Section 201 of the New York City Charter, for an amendment of the Zoning Resolution of the City of New York, amending APPENDIX F for the purpose of establishing a Mandatory Inclusionary Housing Area, Borough of Brooklyn, Community District 1, Council District 33.

Summary

This zoning text amendment establishes a Mandatory Inclusionary Housing area at 200 Kent Avenue in Williamsburg, Brooklyn, alongside a related zoning map change. It would support converting and expanding an existing five-story commercial building into a 14-story mixed-use development with 143 dwelling units, of which 29 to 43 would be permanently income-restricted. The Council approved the City Planning Commission's decision with modifications on August 13, 2026.
