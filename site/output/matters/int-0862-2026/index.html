Law to require 600 first-time homebuyer grants yearly

Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to establishing a homebuyer financial assistance program and mandating a minimum number of homebuyers to be assisted annually

Summary

This bill would require the Department of Housing Preservation and Development to provide financial assistance to at least 600 first-time homebuyers annually through its HomeFirst down payment program. HPD would have to submit an annual report, starting July 31, 2027, on the number of recipients, median assistance amount and community districts of homes purchased, and explain any shortfall below the 600-person target. The requirement applies beginning with the fiscal year starting July 1, 2026.
