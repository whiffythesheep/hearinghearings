Official title

A Local Law in relation to requiring the city to report on the impact of social media on the mental health of young people

Summary

This bill would require the Department of Youth and Community Development, working with the Department of Health and Mental Hygiene, to report annually for five years on how social media affects young people's mental health. The report would identify the most-used platforms, the uses causing the greatest harm, and recommendations on appropriate starting age and daily or weekly time limits for youth social media use. It would also address the negative effects of advertising that targets youth on social media.
