Zoning text change for 21-31 46th Avenue, Queens

· Sponsor: Kevin C. Riley · Decided by Res 0678-2026 ↗ · View on Legistar ↗

Official title

Application number N 250126 ZRQ (21-31 46th Ave Rezoning) submitted by 21-31 Holdings LLC, pursuant to Section 201 of the New York City Charter, for an amendment of the Zoning Resolution of the City of New York, amending APPENDIX F for the purpose of establishing a Mandatory Inclusionary Housing Area, Borough of Queens, Community District 2, Council District 26.

Summary

This application would establish a Mandatory Inclusionary Housing area for a site at 21-31 46th Avenue in Long Island City, Queens, paired with a related zoning map change. Together the actions would allow an 11-story mixed-use building with about 84 apartments, including 21 income-restricted units, and ground-floor commercial space. The Council held a public hearing on September 9, 2026 and approved the measure with modifications.
