Resolution

Res 0627-2026

Resolution urging Albany to create disability housing waitlist

In committee · Sponsor: Frank Morano · View on Legistar ↗

In brief

This resolution calls on the New York State Legislature to pass, and the Governor to sign, S.4868 and a companion Assembly bill requiring the Office for People with Developmental Disabilities to create and maintain a statewide waitlist for community residence placements. It also calls for increased OPWDD funding to expand residential capacity, with priority given to people with severe and profound developmental disabilities. OPWDD currently serves about 135,000 New Yorkers, including roughly 35,000 people receiving certified residential services.

Official title

Resolution calling on the New York State Legislature to pass, and the Governor to sign, S.4868 and a companion bill in the Assembly, which would require the Office for People with Developmental Disabilities (OPWDD) to develop and maintain a community residence opportunities waitlist, and further calling for increased OPWDD funding to expand residential capacity with priority for individuals with severe and profound developmental disabilities
