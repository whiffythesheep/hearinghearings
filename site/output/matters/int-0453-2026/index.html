Law to add mental health support for kids visiting jail

· Sponsor: Althea V. Stevens
+ 12 co-sponsors Mercedes Narcisse, Kevin C. Riley, Nantasha M. Williams, Chris Banks, Crystal Hudson, Selvena N. Brooks-Powers, Shahana K. Hanif, Sandy Nurse, Tiffany L. Cabán, Virginia Maloney, Harvey D. Epstein, Amanda C. Farías
· View on Legistar ↗

Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to the provision of mental health services for children visiting incarcerated individuals

Summary

This bill would require the Department of Correction to set up a mental health services program at city jails for visitors under 18 years old. The program would provide referrals and, when practical, onsite mental health services to child visitors. Participation would be voluntary, and the law would take effect 120 days after enactment.
