LU 0115-2026 (Passed Council: adopted)

Rezoning map change for 200 Kent Avenue, Brooklyn

· Sponsor: Kevin C. Riley · Decided by Res 0641-2026 ↗ · View on Legistar ↗

Official title

Application number C 260149 ZMK (200 Kent Avenue Rezoning) submitted by 206 Kent LLC and Kent Investor LLC, pursuant to Sections 197-c and 201 of the New York City Charter for an amendment of the Zoning Map, Section No. 12c, changing from an M1-4 District to an M1-4A/R7X District and establishing a Special Mixed Use District (MX-8), Borough of Brooklyn, Community District 1, Council District 33.

Summary

This application would change the zoning map for property at 200 Kent Avenue in Williamsburg, Brooklyn from an M1-4 district to an M1-4A/R7X district and create a Special Mixed Use District. Combined with a related zoning text change, it would allow conversion of an existing five-story commercial building into a 14-story mixed-use development with 143 apartments, including 29 to 43 income-restricted units, and commercial space. The Council approved the change on August 13, 2026.
