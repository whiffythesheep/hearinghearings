Official title

Resolution declaring October 3 as Korean Cultural Awareness Day annually in the City of New York to commemorate the mythological beginning of the Korean people and to celebrate their continued pride in their shared ancestry

Summary

This resolution would declare October 3 as Korean Cultural Awareness Day each year in New York City, marking the mythological founding of the Korean people. It notes about 90,000 Koreans and Korean Americans live in the city, mostly in Queens, Manhattan and Brooklyn. The day is celebrated in South Korea as Gaecheonjeol, or National Foundation Day.
