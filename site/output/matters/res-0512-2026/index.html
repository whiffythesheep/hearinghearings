Resolution urging Albany to protect rent freeze benefits after income spikes

· Sponsor: Susan Zhuang
+ 6 co-sponsors Virginia Maloney, Althea V. Stevens, Farah N. Louis, Lynn C. Schulman, Shirley Aldebol, Frank Morano
· View on Legistar ↗

Official title

Resolution calling upon the New York State Legislature to pass, and the Governor to sign, S.4252/A.10721, to provide for a continuation of senior citizen rent increase exemption or disability rent increase exemption benefits after a period of ineligibility due to a head of household's temporary increase in income

Summary

This resolution calls on the New York State Legislature to pass, and the Governor to sign, S.4252/A.10721, which would let seniors and people with disabilities keep their SCRIE or DRIE rent freeze benefits after a temporary increase in household income. It aims to stop one-time income spikes, such as pension adjustments or emergency withdrawals, from causing loss of these rent protections. The bill has already passed the State Senate and is pending in the Assembly.
