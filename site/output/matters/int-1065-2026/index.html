Int 1065-2026 (In committee: heard, awaiting a vote)

Law to set cost benchmarks for small capital projects

· Sponsor: Carl Wilson
+ 3 co-sponsors Ty Hankerson, Kamillah Hanks, Frank Morano
· View on Legistar ↗

Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to requiring cost benchmarks for small recurring capital projects

Summary

This bill would require the Department of Design and Construction to establish and maintain cost benchmarks for small recurring capital projects like standard city structures, updating them at least every two years. The department would report annually, starting each January 1, comparing actual project costs to these benchmarks and post the findings online.
