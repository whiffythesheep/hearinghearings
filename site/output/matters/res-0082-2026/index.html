Official title

Resolution celebrating the Feast of San Gennaro and the Ferragosto Festival annually in September to honor the contributions of Italian immigrants and Italian Americans to the cultural, political, and economic fabric of the City of New York

Summary

This resolution celebrates the Feast of San Gennaro and the Ferragosto Festival, held annually in September in Manhattan's Little Italy and the Bronx's Arthur Avenue, to honor Italian immigrants and Italian Americans. It cites that New York City has just over 500,000 residents who identify as Italian, the largest European ethnic group in the city. The resolution does not create any new law or funding but formally recognizes both festivals.
