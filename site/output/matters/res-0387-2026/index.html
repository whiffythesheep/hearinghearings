Resolution

Res 0387-2026

Resolution urging Albany to pass bills easing affordable housing insurance costs

Law or adopted · Sponsors: Gale A. Brewer, Farah N. Louis, Virginia Maloney, Lynn C. Schulman, Chris Banks, Mercedes Narcisse, Carl Wilson, Amanda C. Farías, Eric Dinowitz · View on Legistar ↗

In brief

This resolution calls on the New York State Legislature to pass, and the Governor to sign, A.07828A/S.07939, A.9015 and A.9016A. Together these bills would create an emergency insurance relief fund for affordable housing developers, set up a task force to study affordable housing insurance issues and require state agencies to publish yearly reports on affordable housing insurance premiums, denials and claims. The Council cites data showing affordable housing insurance premiums rose 103 percent between 2019 and 2024.

Official title

Resolution calling on the New York State Legislature to pass, and the Governor to sign, A.07828A/S.07939, A.9015 and A.9016A to mitigate the rising cost of property insurance for affordable housing
