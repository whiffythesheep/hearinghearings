Int 1088-2026 (In committee: heard, awaiting a vote)

Law to require an information toolkit for older adults

· Sponsor: Darlene Mealy
+ 6 co-sponsors Virginia Maloney, Tiffany L. Cabán, Christopher Marte, Mercedes Narcisse, Jennifer Gutiérrez, Frank Morano
· View on Legistar ↗

Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to an informational toolkit on available programs, benefits, and services for older adults

Summary

This bill would require the Department for the Aging to create an informational toolkit on programs, benefits and services available to New Yorkers age 60 and older, including rent freeze and meal delivery programs. DFTA would have to distribute printed copies annually at older adult centers and to homebound seniors, post a digital version online, and update the toolkit regularly. The toolkit must be available in English and all designated citywide languages and include accessibility features.
