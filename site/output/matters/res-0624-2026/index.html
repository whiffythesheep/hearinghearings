Official title

Resolution designating October 17 annually as Wyclef Jean Day in the City of New York to honor his musical and philanthropic contributions as a globally recognized artist and proud son of Haiti whose impact resonates deeply in New York City and beyond

Summary

This resolution designates October 17 each year as Wyclef Jean Day in New York City. It honors the Haitian-born musician's career with the Fugees and as a solo artist, his GRAMMY awards and his philanthropic and music education work in Brooklyn and beyond.
