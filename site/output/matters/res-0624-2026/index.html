Resolution declaring October 17 Wyclef Jean Day

In committee · Sponsors: Farah N. Louis, Rita C. Joseph, Mercedes Narcisse, Lincoln Restler, Shahana K. Hanif · View on Legistar ↗

In brief

This resolution designates October 17 annually as Wyclef Jean Day in New York City. It honors the Haitian-born musician's career, including his work with the Fugees, his GRAMMY awards and his philanthropic efforts in Haiti and New York City schools.

Official title

Resolution designating October 17 annually as Wyclef Jean Day in the City of New York to honor his musical and philanthropic contributions as a globally recognized artist and proud son of Haiti whose impact resonates deeply in New York City and beyond
