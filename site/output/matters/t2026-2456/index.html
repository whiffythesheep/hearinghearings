Official title

Oversight - Mental Health Care in New York City Jails.

Summary

The Council's Committee on Criminal Justice held an oversight hearing examining mental health care in New York City jails, where about 60% of the jail population has documented mental health needs and 22% have a serious mental illness diagnosis. The committee also heard three bills: one extending a task force on transgender and gender nonconforming people in custody, one requiring mental health services for children visiting incarcerated relatives, and one requiring Correction to address medical needs during and after lock-ins. Data reviewed showed only about 55-56% of scheduled mental health appointments in jails were completed in mid-2026.
