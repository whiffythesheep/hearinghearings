Law to require an FDNY fire emergency dashboard

Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to the creation of an FDNY fire emergency dashboard

Summary

This bill would require the Fire Department to run a public online dashboard showing data on fire emergencies, including approximate location, date, time and cause for each incident over the prior two years. Users could filter the data by borough, time period and cause. The dashboard would need to be updated at least once a month and the law would take effect 180 days after enactment.
