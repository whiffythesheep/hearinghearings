Resolution urging feds to halt Skyport seaplane flights

· Sponsor: Virginia Maloney
+ 1 co-sponsor Lincoln Restler
· View on Legistar ↗

Official title

Resolution calling on the National Transportation Safety Board and the Federal Aviation Administration to suspend all flights of seaplane operations at the New York City Skyport Seaplane Terminal until a comprehensive safety review of such operations is completed.

Summary

This resolution calls on the National Transportation Safety Board and the Federal Aviation Administration to suspend all seaplane flights at the Skyport Seaplane Terminal on Manhattan's East Side until a full safety review is completed. It cites a July 5, 2026 hard landing of a seaplane in the East River that injured two passengers and required a Fire Department rescue of all eight people aboard. The terminal recorded 2,719 enplanements in 2025.
