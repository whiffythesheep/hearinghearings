Int 0839-2026

Law to make anti-harassment building screening permanent

In brief

This bill would make permanent the Certification of No Harassment pilot program, which requires certain distressed buildings to prove they have not harassed tenants before doing major construction work. It adds new criteria for including buildings on the program list, such as those sharing an owner with a building found to have harassed tenants, and requires HPD to republish the list every three years starting April 15, 2027. It also adds tenant notification requirements and an exception for routine cosmetic maintenance work.

Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to making the certification of no harassment pilot program permanent and adding criteria for inclusion of certain buildings in the program
