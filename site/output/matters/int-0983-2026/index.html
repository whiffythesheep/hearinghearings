Int 0983-2026 (Law: Local Law 134 of 2026)

Law to raise salaries for the mayor, Council and other officials

· Local Law 134 of 2026, enacted August 18, 2026 · Sponsor: Nantasha M. Williams
+ 1 co-sponsor Mercedes Narcisse
· View on Legistar ↗

Official title

A Local Law to amend the New York city charter and the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to compensation of the mayor, public advocate, members of the city council, borough presidents, comptroller, and district attorneys

Summary

This law would raise the salaries of the mayor, public advocate, comptroller, Council members, borough presidents and district attorneys by 18.2 percent, retroactive to January 1, 2026, following a recommendation from the 2026 Quadrennial Advisory Commission. It also moves future compensation reviews to the third year of a term instead of the first, giving each review commission 120 days to complete its work, and adds automatic cost-of-living adjustments if a commission is not convened. New salaries include $305,800 for the mayor, $175,500 for Council members and $251,500 for district attorneys.
