Law to require delivery apps to carry accident insurance

· Sponsor: Gale A. Brewer
+ 6 co-sponsors Shaun Abreu, Harvey D. Epstein, Crystal Hudson, Lincoln Restler, James F. Gennaro, Frank Morano
· View on Legistar ↗

Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to requiring third-party food delivery services to maintain liability insurance

Summary

This bill would require third-party food delivery services to maintain liability insurance covering injuries, deaths or property damage caused by delivery workers using e-bikes or other powered devices during trips or on-call time. Minimum coverage would be $100,000 per person, $300,000 per accident and $50,000 for property damage. A delivery service's license would be voided if it fails to obtain or maintain this insurance, and the law would take effect 180 days after enactment.
