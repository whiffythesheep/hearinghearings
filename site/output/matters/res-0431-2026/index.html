Resolution urging Albany to give tax breaks for senior and homeless housing

+ 5 co-sponsors Nantasha M. Williams, Selvena N. Brooks-Powers, Jennifer Gutiérrez, Farah N. Louis, Kevin C. Riley
· View on Legistar ↗

Official title

Resolution calling upon the New York State Legislature to pass, and the Governor to sign, legislation authorizing property tax incentives for property owners who dedicate twenty percent of existing housing stock to senior citizens and individuals experiencing homelessness

Summary

This resolution calls on the New York State Legislature and Governor to authorize property tax incentives for property owners who set aside 20 percent of their existing housing units for senior citizens and people experiencing homelessness. It cites a 1.4 percent citywide rental vacancy rate in 2023 and roughly 105,000 people in city shelters in mid-2025 as evidence of the need. No such state or local tax incentive program currently exists for this purpose.
