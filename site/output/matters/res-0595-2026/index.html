Resolution to update groups receiving City budget funding

· Sponsor: Linda Lee · View on Legistar ↗

Official title

Resolution approving the new designation and changes in the designation of certain organizations to receive funding in the Expense Budget

Summary

This resolution approves new and changed designations of organizations that receive discretionary funding under the City's Fiscal Year 2026 and 2027 Expense Budgets, covering dozens of programs from senior services to violence prevention. It also amends the funding purposes for some organizations. Some listed groups have not yet completed required prequalification review, and some funding will not take effect until a budget modification passes.
