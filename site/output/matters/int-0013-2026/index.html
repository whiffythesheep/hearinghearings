Int 0013-2026 (Law: Local Law 103 of 2026)

Law to require solar-powered crosswalk signals citywide

· Enacted June 13, 2026 · Sponsor: Selvena N. Brooks-Powers
+ 13 co-sponsors Lincoln Restler, Julie Won, Mercedes Narcisse, Kamillah Hanks, Farah N. Louis, Gale A. Brewer, Crystal Hudson, Shahana K. Hanif, Phil Wong, Christopher Marte, Julie Menin, Tiffany L. Cabán, Chris Banks
· View on Legistar ↗

Official title

A Local Law in relation to the installation of illuminated and solar-powered traffic devices at crosswalks

Summary

This bill would require the Department of Transportation to install at least 500 illuminated, solar-powered traffic control devices at crosswalks over five years, at a rate of at least 100 per year. DOT would also have to study how effective these devices are compared to non-illuminated signs and report its findings to the Council Speaker within two years. The law would take effect immediately.
