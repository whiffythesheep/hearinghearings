Int 0936-2026 (In committee: heard, awaiting a vote)

Law to create a City Department of Coastal Protection

· Sponsor: James F. Gennaro
+ 4 co-sponsors Farah N. Louis, Lincoln Restler, Lynn C. Schulman, Justin E. Sanchez
· View on Legistar ↗

Official title

A Local Law to amend the New York city charter, in relation to the establishment of a department of coastal protection

Summary

This bill would establish a new Department of Coastal Protection, led by a Commissioner, to develop policies and infrastructure programs protecting the city's shoreline from storms, erosion and climate change. The Commissioner could receive and spend funds for coastal protection, issue reports, make rules and run public education efforts. The law would take effect 180 days after enactment.
