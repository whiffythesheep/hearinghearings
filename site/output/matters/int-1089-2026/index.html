Law to create an office helping 9/11 responders and survivors

· Sponsor: Frank Morano

· View on Legistar ↗

Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to creating an office of assistance for 9/11 first responders and survivors

Summary

This bill would create an Office of Assistance for 9/11 First Responders and Survivors within the Department of Social Services to connect eligible people with benefits, services and case management. The office must give applicants a written receipt on their request, publish a bill of rights, and issue an annual report on outcomes like wait times and approval rates. The director must also create a staff policy manual and quality assurance procedures within 60 days of the law taking effect.
