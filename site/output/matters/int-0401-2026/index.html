Int 0401-2026 (In committee: heard, awaiting a vote)

Law to require public compost bins citywide

· Sponsor: Lincoln Restler
+ 3 co-sponsors Farah N. Louis, Crystal Hudson, Harvey D. Epstein
· View on Legistar ↗

Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to public organic waste receptacles.

Summary

This bill would require the Department of Sanitation to install at least 25 public organic waste receptacles in every community district, prioritizing commercial corridors, parks, transit hubs and other high-pedestrian areas. The agency would have to empty these bins at least once a week and post their locations on its website. The law would take effect 120 days after enactment.
