Int 0739-2026 (In committee: heard, awaiting a vote)

Law to require city agencies to train staff on immigration rules

· Sponsor: Shahana K. Hanif
+ 9 co-sponsors Alexa Avilés, Lincoln Restler, Tiffany L. Cabán, Gale A. Brewer, Jennifer Gutiérrez, Carmen N. De La Rosa, Farah N. Louis, Shanel Thomas-Henry, Rita C. Joseph
· View on Legistar ↗

Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to requiring agency training and the publication of implementation plans concerning immigration enforcement and cooperation with non-local law enforcement agencies

Summary

This bill would require city agencies, including the NYPD, Department of Correction and Department of Probation, to train employees, contractors and subcontractors on existing laws limiting cooperation with immigration enforcement and non-local law enforcement requests. Agencies would also have to publish implementation plans and compliance protocols explaining how staff should follow these laws, including policies on responding to requests from federal immigration authorities. These requirements would take effect by January 1, 2027.
