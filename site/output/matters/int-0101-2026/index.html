Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to CityFHEPS application response times

Summary

This bill would require the Department of Social Services to tell CityFHEPS rental assistance applicants within 15 days whether their application is approved, denied, or needs more information. It would take effect 90 days after becoming law.
