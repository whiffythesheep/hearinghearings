LU 0118-2026 (Passed Council: adopted)

Zoning map change for 47-03 108th Street, Corona

· Sponsor: Kevin C. Riley · Decided by Res 0643-2026 ↗ · View on Legistar ↗

Official title

Application number C 260147 ZMQ (47-03 108th Street Rezoning) submitted by 108 Realty Group Inc., pursuant to Sections 197-c and 201 of the New York City Charter for an amendment of the Zoning Map, Section No. 10b, changing from an R6B District to an R7X District and establishing within the proposed R7X District a C2-4 District, Borough of Queens, Community District 4, Council District 21.

Summary

This rezones a site at 47-03 108th Street in Corona, Queens from R6B to R7D with a C2-4 overlay, as modified by the Council from the original R7X proposal. It would allow a 13-story mixed-use building with about 119,500 square feet of floor area, roughly 119 apartments (30 income-restricted), plus commercial and community facility space. The City Planning Commission's decision was approved with modifications on August 13, 2026.
