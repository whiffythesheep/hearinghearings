Int 1068-2026 (In committee: awaiting a hearing)

Law to excuse TLC drivers helping disabled riders from tickets

· Sponsor: Susan Zhuang
+ 7 co-sponsors Frank Morano, Farah N. Louis, Virginia Maloney, Ty Hankerson, Kevin C. Riley, Shirley Aldebol, Inna Vernikov
· View on Legistar ↗

Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to an affirmative defense for tickets issued to drivers licensed by the taxi and limousine commission for stopping and standing while assisting passengers with impaired mobility

Summary

This bill would let taxi and for-hire vehicle drivers avoid stopping or standing tickets if they were briefly helping a passenger with impaired mobility get in or out of the vehicle. The defense would not apply if the driver blocked a fire hydrant, emergency vehicle, bus or bike lane, median, intersection or crosswalk, or stopped for more than 10 minutes. The law would take effect 180 days after being enacted.
