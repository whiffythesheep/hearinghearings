Int 1099-2026 (In committee: heard, awaiting a vote)

Law to require paid-for disclosures on political social media posts

· Sponsor: Phil Wong
+ 2 co-sponsors David M. Carr, Frank Morano
· View on Legistar ↗

Official title

A Local Law to amend the New York city charter and the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to requiring campaign finance disclosures in content posted on internet websites, digital applications, and social media platforms, and directing the campaign finance board to issue guidance regarding the disclosure, recordkeeping, and reporting requirements applicable to such content

Summary

This bill would require campaigns and independent spenders to include "paid for by" disclosures in political content posted on websites, apps and social media, similar to rules for other ads. It also directs the Campaign Finance Board to issue guidance on how disclosure, recordkeeping and reporting rules apply to such online content, including posts by paid third parties. The law would take effect immediately upon passage.
