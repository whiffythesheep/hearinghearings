Int 1075-2026 (In committee: heard, awaiting a vote)

Law to bar candidates from taxpayer-funded ads near elections

· Sponsor: David M. Carr
+ 2 co-sponsors Phil Wong, Frank Morano
· View on Legistar ↗

Official title

A Local Law to amend the New York city charter, in relation to prohibiting candidates from appearing in certain government-funded third-party communications during an election campaign

Summary

This bill would amend the City Charter to prohibit a public servant who is running for elected office, or their spouse, from appearing in any government-funded third-party public communication, such as social media posts, during the 90 days before a primary or general election. It defines third-party public communication as content whose author is not a public servant or agency, or is not clearly identified as one. The law would take effect immediately upon passage.
