Int 1069-2026 (In committee: heard, awaiting a vote)

Law to study and set safe delivery time rules

· Sponsor: Shaun Abreu
+ 1 co-sponsor Lincoln Restler
· By request of the Mayor · View on Legistar ↗

Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to the study of safe delivery times by contracted delivery workers and the promulgation of safe delivery time standards

Summary

This bill requires delivery services to submit 24 months of historical trip data to the Department of Consumer and Worker Protection and directs the department to study how delivery services' scheduling and assignment practices affect street safety. Based on that study, the department must create rules setting safe delivery time standards, which may limit incentives or penalties tied to delivery speed. The law would take effect alongside a related bill on delivery data retention.
