Int 0846-2026 (In committee: heard, awaiting a vote)

Law to require ferry service from Willets Point to Manhattan

· Sponsor: Shanel Thomas-Henry
+ 11 co-sponsors Virginia Maloney, Ty Hankerson, Althea V. Stevens, Sandra Ung, Harvey D. Epstein, Selvena N. Brooks-Powers, Julie Won, Jennifer Gutiérrez, Yusef Salaam, Lynn C. Schulman, Kevin C. Riley
· View on Legistar ↗

Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to ferry service to Willets Point

Summary

This bill would require the city's Department of Transportation to run regular public ferry service connecting Willets Point in Queens to at least Midtown and the Financial District in Manhattan. The commissioner would set the schedule and may charge fees, which would be posted online. The law would take effect 180 days after being enacted.
