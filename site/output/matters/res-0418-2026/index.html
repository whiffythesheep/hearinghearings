Official title

Resolution calling on The City University of New York to ensure that all campuses award an appropriate number of college credits to students who earned a New York State Seal of Civic Readiness in high school

Summary

This resolution calls on CUNY to ensure all its campuses award an appropriate number of college credits to students who earned the New York State Seal of Civic Readiness in high school. It notes CUNY already awards three to eight credits for the similar Seal of Biliteracy. In 2024-2025, 7,855 students across 166 Department of Education high schools earned the civic readiness seal, a 72 percent increase over the prior year.
