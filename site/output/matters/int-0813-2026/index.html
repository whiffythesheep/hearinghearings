Law to revoke tobacco licenses for illegal cannabis sales

Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to refusing to renew, suspending, or revoking a license of a tobacco retail dealer or an electronic cigarette retail dealer that has violated certain authorization requirements of the cannabis law

Summary

This bill would let the city's Department of Consumer and Worker Protection refuse to renew, suspend or revoke a tobacco or e-cigarette retail dealer's license if the holder is found to have sold cannabis or cannabis products without the required state license or permit. The agency would also have to conduct outreach to inform affected licensees of this new basis for losing their license. The law would take effect immediately upon passage.
