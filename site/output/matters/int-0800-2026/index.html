Int 0800-2026 (In committee: heard, awaiting a vote)

Law to make 311 forward complaints to the right agency

· Sponsor: Lynn C. Schulman
+ 8 co-sponsors Farah N. Louis, Sandy Nurse, Carmen N. De La Rosa, Selvena N. Brooks-Powers, Gale A. Brewer, Shirley Aldebol, Joann Ariola, Frank Morano
· View on Legistar ↗

Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to requiring the 311 customer service center to redirect requests for service or complaints to the appropriate agency

Summary

This bill would require NYC311 to redirect service requests or complaints to the agency with proper jurisdiction when an agency reports it cannot act on one. NYC311 would have to reopen the request, determine the correct agency and forward it there, and notify the person who filed it. The same process would apply when someone asks to reopen a closed request through a satisfaction survey. The law would take effect 180 days after enactment.
