Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to establishing a support fund for small retail businesses affected by roadway construction

Summary

This bill would require the Department of Small Business Services to run a program offering loans, grants, services or materials to small businesses affected by city construction projects lasting 30 days or more. Loans would be capped at $50,000 per storefront per year with interest no higher than 3 percent, and grants or reimbursements capped at $15,000 per storefront per year. The agency would need to decide on applications within 30 days and pay out approved awards within 14 days.
