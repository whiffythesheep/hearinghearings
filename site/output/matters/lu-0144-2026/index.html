LU 0144-2026 (Passed Council: adopted)

Rezoning to allow housing at 135-27 Sapphire Street, Queens

· Sponsor: Kevin C. Riley · Decided by Res 0713-2026 ↗ · View on Legistar ↗

Official title

Application number C 250329 ZMQ (135-27 Sapphire Street Rezoning) submitted by 135 Sapphire LLC, pursuant to Sections 197-c and 201 of the New York City Charter for an amendment of the Zoning Map, Section No. 18b, changing from an R4 District to an R6A District, Borough of Queens, Community District 10, Council District 32.

Summary

This application changes the zoning map from an R4 to an R6A district for property at 135-27 Sapphire Street in Queens Community District 10. The change would allow construction of a new six-story residential building of about 186,380 square feet with roughly 268 apartments, including about 67 income-restricted units under Mandatory Inclusionary Housing. The City Planning Commission approved the application and the Council held a public hearing on September 23, 2026.
