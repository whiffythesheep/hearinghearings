Resolution

Res 0237-2026

Resolution urging Congress to raise tax break for performing artists

Law or adopted · Sponsors: Crystal Hudson, Chi A. Ossé, Farah N. Louis, Tiffany L. Cabán, Frank Morano · View on Legistar ↗

In brief

This resolution calls on the U.S. Congress to pass, and the President to sign, the Performing Artist Tax Parity Act of 2025. The act would raise the income cap for a tax deduction available to qualifying performing artists from $16,000, a figure unchanged for nearly 40 years, to $100,000 for individuals and $200,000 for joint filers. It would also tie the cap to inflation going forward.
