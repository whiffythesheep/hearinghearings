Law to tighten pedicab rules on meters, signs and training

· Sponsor: Gale A. Brewer
+ 3 co-sponsors Carl Wilson, Julie Won, (in conjunction with the Manhattan Borough Preside
· View on Legistar ↗

Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to making certain changes to the pedicab law

Summary

This bill would require pedicabs to use meters instead of timers and registration plates with scannable codes showing the pedicab's registration number and business name. It would require the Department of Consumer and Worker Protection to issue official signs, create a required safety training course for drivers, and let drivers pick up licenses in person. It would also drop jail time as a penalty for pedicab violations and ban drivers from using sound devices that create illegal noise.
