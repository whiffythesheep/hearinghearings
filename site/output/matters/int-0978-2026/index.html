Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to increasing the maximum income level qualifying for exemption from rent increases granted to certain senior citizens and persons with disabilities and adjusting the income requirements related thereto

Summary

This bill would raise the maximum household income allowed to qualify for the Senior Citizen Rent Increase Exemption and Disability Rent Increase Exemption programs to $75,000, beginning July 1, 2026. It would also align the definition of income used for eligibility with New York State law. The change applies retroactively to July 1, 2026.
