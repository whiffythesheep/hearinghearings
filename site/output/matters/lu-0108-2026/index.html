Land use

LU 0108-2026

Rezoning to allow taller housing at 1166 Bedford Avenue, Brooklyn

Passed Council · Sponsor: Kevin C. Riley · View on Legistar ↗

In brief

This zoning map amendment changes property near 1166 Bedford Avenue in Bedford-Stuyvesant, Brooklyn from an R6A to an R7X district. Combined with a related text amendment, it would allow a new 12-story mixed-use building with about 144 income-restricted apartments, 13,400 square feet of community facility space and 4,800 square feet of ground-floor commercial space. The City Council approved the change on July 14, 2026.

Official title

Application number C 260162 ZMK (1166 Bedford Avenue Rezoning) submitted by Khalifah Residences LLC, pursuant to Sections 197-c and 201 of the New York City Charter for an amendment of the Zoning Map, Section No. 17a, changing from an R6A District to an R7X District, Borough of Brooklyn, Community District 3, Council District 36.
