Int 1010-2026 (In committee: heard, awaiting a vote)

Law to raise income limits for senior and disabled homeowner tax breaks

· Sponsor: Christopher Marte
+ 15 co-sponsors Phil Wong, Susan Zhuang, Harvey D. Epstein, Gale A. Brewer, Farah N. Louis, Crystal Hudson, Shahana K. Hanif, Virginia Maloney, Ty Hankerson, Lynn C. Schulman, Kevin C. Riley, Lincoln Restler, Frank Morano, Joann Ariola, Public Advocate Jumaane Williams
· View on Legistar ↗

Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to increasing the maximum income level qualifying for the senior citizen homeowner’s exemption and the disabled homeowner’s exemption

Summary

This bill would raise the income threshold for the Senior Citizen Homeowners' Exemption (SCHE) and Disabled Homeowners' Exemption (DHE) from $50,000 to $75,000 starting July 1, 2027, with updated sliding-scale exemption schedules. It would also raise the maximum SCHE exemption from 50 percent to 65 percent. Under the new schedule, homeowners earning under $72,000 could get a 65 percent exemption, while those earning up to $83,400 could still get a partial exemption.
