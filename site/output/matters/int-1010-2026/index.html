Proposed law

Int 1010-2026

Law to raise income limit for senior and disabled homeowner tax breaks

In committee · Sponsors: Christopher Marte, Phil Wong, Susan Zhuang, Harvey D. Epstein, Gale A. Brewer, Farah N. Louis, Crystal Hudson, Shahana K. Hanif, Virginia Maloney, Ty Hankerson, Lynn C. Schulman, Frank Morano, Public Advocate Jumaane Williams · View on Legistar ↗

In brief

This bill would raise the maximum household income allowed to qualify for the Senior Citizen Homeowners' Exemption and the Disabled Homeowners' Exemption from $50,000 to $75,000, starting July 1, 2027. It also updates the sliding-scale income brackets used to determine the size of each homeowner's partial property tax exemption. The exemptions reduce the taxable assessed value of homes owned by eligible low-income seniors and people with disabilities.

Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to increasing the maximum income level qualifying for the senior citizen homeowner’s exemption and the disabled homeowner’s exemption
