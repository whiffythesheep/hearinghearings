Official title

Oversight - Community-based Prevention and Response to Hate and Bias.

Summary

The Committee to Combat Hate held an oversight hearing on how city offices and community organizations work together to prevent and respond to hate crimes. Testimony came from the Mayor's Office for the Prevention of Hate Crimes, the Mayor's Office to Combat Antisemitism, the Mayor's Office of Faith-Based and Community Partnerships, advocates and the public. The hearing examined 2025 hate crime trends, which showed an overall 20 percent citywide drop in complaints but sharp increases for some groups such as Buddhist communities and women, and reviewed community-based prevention programs like PATH Forward and the Citywide Clergy Collective.
