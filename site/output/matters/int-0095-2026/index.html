Int 0095-2026 (In committee: heard, awaiting a vote)

Law to require moped sellers to give safety info

· Sponsor: Gale A. Brewer
+ 8 co-sponsors Julie Won, Farah N. Louis, Susan Zhuang, Lynn C. Schulman, Tiffany L. Cabán, Shirley Aldebol, Frank Morano, Vickie Paladino
· View on Legistar ↗

Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to requiring moped retailers to provide ownership information at point of sale

Summary

This bill would require the Department of Consumer and Worker Protection, working with the Department of Transportation, to create materials on moped registration, inspection, insurance and safe operation. Moped retailers selling more than five mopeds a year would have to hand out these materials to customers at the point of sale. Retailers who fail to do so would face civil penalties ranging from $0 for a first violation to up to $500 for a third or later violation.
