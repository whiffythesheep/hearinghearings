Official title

Oversight – Examining Screen Time and the Use of Digital Devices in New York City Public Schools

Summary

The Council's Oversight and Education Committees will examine how the Department of Education uses digital devices, screens and AI tools in city schools, including new 2026-27 screen time caps and a one-year moratorium on student-facing generative AI for grades 2K-8. The hearing will also review DOE's technology procurement practices, past device purchases such as the $320 million Chromebook contract, and compliance with the state's new 'Distraction-Free Schools' cellphone ban. Witnesses will include DOE officials, tech industry and advocacy groups, unions, parents, students and educators.
