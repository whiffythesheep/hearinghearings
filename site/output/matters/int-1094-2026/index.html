Int 1094-2026 (In committee: heard, awaiting a vote)

Law requiring faster reporting of Legionella test results

· Sponsor: Lynn C. Schulman
+ 3 co-sponsors Virginia Maloney, Linda Lee, Farah N. Louis
· View on Legistar ↗

Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to reporting Legionella test results to the department of health and mental hygiene

Summary

This bill would require building owners to notify the city Health Department within 24 hours if a Legionella test of a cooling tower shows 10 or more colony-forming units per milliliter. Owners would also be required to take corrective action under department rules. The law would take effect immediately upon passage.
