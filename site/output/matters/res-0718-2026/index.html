Official title

Resolution endorsing the boycott of Starbucks called for by Workers United-SEIU and encouraging City residents and agencies to refrain from purchasing Starbucks products until Starbucks finalizes a fair union contract

Summary

This resolution endorses the Starbucks boycott called for by Starbucks Workers United-SEIU and urges New York City residents and agencies to stop buying Starbucks products until the company finalizes a fair union contract. It cites unresolved contract talks since April 2024 and a November 2025 strike involving more than 4,500 baristas at about 230 stores. It also notes a December 2025 city settlement requiring Starbucks to pay $38.9 million to more than 15,000 workers for Fair Workweek Law violations.
