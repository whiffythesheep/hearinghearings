Int 0860-2026 (In committee: heard, awaiting a vote)

Law to require inspections of building street numbers

· Sponsor: Gale A. Brewer
+ 4 co-sponsors Farah N. Louis, Shirley Aldebol, Harvey D. Epstein, Frank Morano
· View on Legistar ↗

Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to the inspection and enforcement of street numbers on buildings

Summary

This bill would require HPD and the FDNY to check for posted street numbers during their routine building inspections and to take enforcement action under Department of City Planning rules when numbers are missing or non-compliant. It would also require either agency to inspect a building within 60 days after a 311 complaint about a missing or improper street number. The law would take effect on January 1, 2027.
