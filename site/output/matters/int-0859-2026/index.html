Int 0859-2026 (Law: Local Law 122 of 2026)

Law to require research when street or park renaming lacks biography

· Local Law 122 of 2026, enacted July 30, 2026 · Sponsor: Gale A. Brewer
+ 10 co-sponsors Selvena N. Brooks-Powers, Crystal Hudson, Farah N. Louis, Shahana K. Hanif, Lincoln Restler, Shirley Aldebol, Amanda C. Farías, Chris Banks, Carl Wilson, Frank Morano
· View on Legistar ↗

Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to the online publication of new biographical information relating to past local laws that changed the name of streets or parks

Summary

This bill directs the city agency responsible for posting biographical information about people honored through street or park renamings to research and draft that information itself when the legislative record does not contain it. The agency must cite its sources and note online when such information cannot be found. The law would take effect 90 days after enactment.
