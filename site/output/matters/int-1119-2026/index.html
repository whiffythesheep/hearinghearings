Int 1119-2026 (In committee: heard, awaiting a vote)

Law requiring a city AI emergency response plan

· Sponsor: Chi A. Ossé
+ 8 co-sponsors Kayla Santosuosso, Virginia Maloney, Farah N. Louis, Julie Won, Tiffany L. Cabán, Mercedes Narcisse, Gale A. Brewer, Shahana K. Hanif
· View on Legistar ↗

Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to requiring the creation of an artificial intelligence model emergency response plan

Summary

This bill would require the Office of Technology and Innovation's Cyber Command and NYC Emergency Management to create or update a plan to detect, prepare for and respond to emergencies caused by artificial intelligence models within 120 days of the law taking effect. The plan would be reviewed and updated annually, and the agencies would submit yearly progress reports on AI emergencies and plan implementation to the Mayor and Council Speaker.
