Introduction

Int 0917-2026

Law to require clean sidewalk and roadway cafe setups

Law or adopted · Sponsors: Justin E. Sanchez, Farah N. Louis, Virginia Maloney, Phil Wong, Lynn C. Schulman, Selvena N. Brooks-Powers, Jennifer Gutiérrez, Frank Morano, Joann Ariola · View on Legistar ↗

In brief

This bill would require operators of roadway and sidewalk cafes to keep their outdoor setups clear of trash, debris, graffiti, vermin, food scraps and unsanitary conditions, including within an 18-inch radius of the cafe. Violations would carry civil penalties of $300 for a first offense and $600 for a second or later offense at the same location. The law would take effect 120 days after passage.

Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to sanitation and cleanliness requirements for roadway and sidewalk cafes
