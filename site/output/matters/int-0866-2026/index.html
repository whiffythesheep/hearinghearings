Introduction

Int 0866-2026

Law to require signs warning of upcoming street work

Awaiting the Mayor · Sponsors: Harvey D. Epstein, Selvena N. Brooks-Powers, Farah N. Louis, Sandra Ung, Shirley Aldebol, Chris Banks, Lynn C. Schulman, Phil Wong, Susan Zhuang, Kevin C. Riley, Alexa Avilés, Mercedes Narcisse, James F. Gennaro, Virginia Maloney, Kamillah Hanks, Shahana K. Hanif, Jennifer Gutiérrez, Ty Hankerson, Frank Morano, Joann Ariola · View on Legistar ↗

In brief

This bill would require the Department of Transportation or its permittees to post signs at least 48 hours before disruptive work like street closures, parking space closures or overnight noisy work lasting 7 days or more. Signs would be posted within 500 feet of the work site and list the contact information of the entity doing the work along with its start date, end date, location and nature. The law would take effect 120 days after being enacted.

Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to requiring the posting of notification signs regarding upcoming disruptive work
