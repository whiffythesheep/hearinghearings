Int 1090-2026 (In committee: heard, awaiting a vote)

Law to create an online portal for landlords in rental aid programs

· Sponsor: Frank Morano
+ 1 co-sponsor Crystal Hudson
· View on Legistar ↗

Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to a landlord portal for rental assistance programs

Summary

This bill would require the Department of Social Services to create an online portal for landlords participating in rental subsidy programs such as FHEPS and CityFHEPS by January 1, 2027. The portal would let landlords upload required documents, enroll for electronic payments, and submit complaints about administrative issues. DSS would have to publish an annual report on landlord complaints and resolution times, starting by December 31, 2028.
