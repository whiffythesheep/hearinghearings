Proposed law

Int 0869-2026 (In committee: heard, awaiting a vote)

Law to set a code of conduct for noise complaint filers

· Sponsor: James F. Gennaro · View on Legistar ↗

Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to establishing a code of conduct applicable to citizen noise complaints

Summary

This bill would direct the Department of Environmental Protection to create rules setting standards of conduct for people who file citizen noise complaints, including requiring dignified behavior and familiarity with the relevant law. The department could disqualify a person from filing complaints if they violate the code, after notice and a chance to be heard. The law would take effect 120 days after enactment.
