Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to giving qualified entities a first opportunity to submit a statement of interest and a first opportunity to purchase certain properties

Summary

This bill would let HPD-certified nonprofits, or joint ventures they control, get first notice and the first chance to make an offer when certain distressed residential properties go up for sale. Owners would have to notify HPD and these qualified entities before selling, giving them 20 days to express interest and 70 days to make an offer, plus a right of first refusal if another buyer makes an offer. The law applies to properties with chronic code violations, expiring affordability restrictions or other distress criteria, and owners could seek a hardship exemption.
