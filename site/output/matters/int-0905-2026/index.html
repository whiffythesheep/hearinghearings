Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to giving qualified entities a first opportunity to submit a statement of interest and a first opportunity to purchase certain properties

Summary

This bill would give HPD-certified nonprofits or nonprofit-led joint ventures a first opportunity to express interest in and purchase certain distressed residential buildings, such as those in HPD's alternative enforcement program or with high violation rates, before they are sold on the open market. Owners would have to notify HPD and qualified entities before selling, giving them 20 days to submit interest and 70 days to make an offer, plus a right of first refusal if another buyer makes an offer. Owners could seek an exemption for undue financial hardship, and the law would take effect one year after passage.
