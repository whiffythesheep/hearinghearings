Summary

This bill would co-name 103 streets and public places across the five boroughs to honor local residents, civic leaders, veterans and community institutions, based on requests from individual Council Members. It also repeals or corrects several earlier co-naming laws to relocate or revise street signs. The Council's Committee on Parks and Recreation was scheduled to vote on the bill on June 30, 2026.
