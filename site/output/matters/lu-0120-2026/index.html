LU 0120-2026 (Passed Council: adopted)

Rezoning to allow larger stores at 815 Hutchinson River Parkway, Bronx

· Sponsor: Kevin C. Riley · Decided by Res 0659-2026 ↗ · View on Legistar ↗

Official title

Application number C 240161 ZMX (815 Hutchinson River Parkway Rezoning) submitted by MD Hutch Owner LLC, pursuant to Sections 197-c and 201 of the New York City Charter for an amendment of the Zoning Map, Section No. 7a, changing from an M1-2 District to a C8-3 District, Borough of the Bronx, Community District 10, Council District 13.

Summary

This zoning map amendment changes the Hutchinson River Parkway shopping center site in the Bronx from an M1-2 manufacturing district to a C8-3 commercial district. The change would let the existing shopping center lease space to individual retail stores larger than 10,000 square feet. The City Planning Commission issued a negative declaration finding no significant environmental impact, and the Council approved the change on September 24, 2026.
