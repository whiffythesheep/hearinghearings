Official title

Resolution calling on the New York State Legislature to pass, and the Governor to sign, S.1018/A.8440, to amend the education law, in relation to contracts for the transportation of school children

Summary

This resolution calls on the state legislature to pass, and the governor to sign, S.1018/A.8440, which would amend state education law to let the Department of Education include job protections for school bus workers in new contract bids. The bill aims to let experienced bus drivers, mechanics, dispatchers and attendants keep their jobs, wages and benefits when bus contracts change hands. The City's current yellow school bus contracts, covering about 17,500 workers at 52 companies, were extended through the 2027-28 school year, and state legislation would need to pass in 2027 for DOE to rebid them with these protections.
