Oversight

T2026-2229

Hearing on the Mayor's Housing Plan and related housing bills

Oversight topic · View on Legistar ↗

In brief

The Council's Housing and Buildings Committee will examine Mayor Mamdani's Block by Block housing plan, which addresses tenant protections, code enforcement, affordability and homeownership amid a citywide housing shortage. The hearing will also cover several related bills, including measures on conversion assistance, owner identification signs, permit sign-off notices, street number enforcement, tenant purchase opportunities, tax abatements for multiple dwellings, and electronic dwelling registration. Officials from the Department of Housing Preservation and Development and the Department of Buildings are expected to testify.
