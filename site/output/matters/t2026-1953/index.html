Official title

Oversight - Racial Equity at CUNY.

Summary

The Council's Committee on Higher Education held an oversight hearing on racial equity at the City University of New York, examining how the demographics of students, faculty and administrators compare to New York City's population. Data show that Black and Hispanic students are overrepresented relative to citywide demographics but underrepresented among full-time faculty, part-time faculty and executives, while White faculty and administrators are overrepresented compared to White enrollment. The hearing examined CUNY's roughly 237,000 students across 26 campuses and what role the Council might play in improving diversity among its instructional staff.
