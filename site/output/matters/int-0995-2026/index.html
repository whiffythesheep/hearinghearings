Law to require police training on restraining pregnant people

· Sponsor: Selvena N. Brooks-Powers

· View on Legistar ↗

Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to requiring that police officers be trained on laws and policies related to the use of physical restraints on pregnant and postpartum individuals

Summary

This bill would require the NYPD to train officers on laws and policies limiting the use of physical restraints on people in custody who are pregnant or postpartum. Starting January 30, 2027 and annually for five years after, the department must publish a report on training delivery and the number of officers trained or not yet trained. The law would take effect 30 days after being signed.
