Int 1085-2026 (In committee: awaiting a hearing)

Law to raise fees on slow-to-fix problem buildings

· Sponsor: Farah N. Louis
+ 3 co-sponsors Jennifer Gutiérrez, Christopher Marte, Lincoln Restler
· View on Legistar ↗

Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to increasing fees charged to buildings not discharged from the alternative enforcement program within the first four months of an initial notice

Summary

This bill would increase fees charged to landlords of buildings enrolled in the Department of Housing Preservation and Development's Alternative Enforcement Program who fail to fix violations and exit the program within four months of being notified. It sets minimum fees for building-wide inspections, complaint-based inspections and reinspections. The law would take effect 180 days after becoming law.
