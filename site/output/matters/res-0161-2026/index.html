Official title

Resolution calling on the New York State Legislature to pass, and the Governor to sign, A.7415 and a Senate companion bill, which would establish a baby bond pilot program for foster youth

Summary

This resolution calls on the New York State Legislature to pass, and the Governor to sign, Assembly bill A.7415 and a Senate companion, establishing a baby bond pilot program for foster youth. Under the bill 1,500 low-income foster youth ages 8 to 18 with limited family support would receive a one-time deposit of $1,000 to $10,000 depending on age plus $100 monthly contributions until age 25, held in an annuity managed by a nonprofit contracted by the Administration for Children's Services. The funds could not be accessed until maturity.
