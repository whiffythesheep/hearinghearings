Int 0913-2026

Law to raise the age limit for becoming an NYPD officer

Law · Sponsors: Amanda C. Farías, Farah N. Louis, Virginia Maloney, Kevin C. Riley, Mercedes Narcisse, Carl Wilson, Selvena N. Brooks-Powers, Kamillah Hanks, Frank Morano, Joann Ariola, Inna Vernikov · View on Legistar ↗

In brief

This bill would raise the maximum age to take the civil service exam for police officer from 35 to 43 years old. It also lets applicants subtract up to six years from their age for time spent on military duty or terminal leave. The change would take effect immediately and apply retroactively to September 1, 2025.

Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to raising the maximum age for taking a civil service examination to become a police officer
