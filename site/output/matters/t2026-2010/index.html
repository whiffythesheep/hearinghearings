Official title

Oversight - Access to Reproductive Health Care for New Yorkers with Disabilities.

Summary

The Council's Disabilities, Health and Hospitals Committees will jointly examine barriers to reproductive and sexual health care faced by New Yorkers with disabilities, alongside related bills on doula resources, fertility treatment information, pre-implantation testing coverage, disability training for health providers and free medication abortion at city clinics. The hearing will also cover resolutions urging the state to fund IVF tax credits, mandate emergency contraception vending machines on SUNY/CUNY campuses and require insurance coverage of genetic embryo testing. Witnesses will include officials from the Mayor's Office for People with Disabilities, the Health Department and NYC Health + Hospitals.
