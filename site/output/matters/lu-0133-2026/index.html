LU 0133-2026 (Passed Council: adopted)

Zoning map change for Park Avenue, Brooklyn

· Sponsor: Kevin C. Riley · Decided by Res 0681-2026 ↗ · View on Legistar ↗

Official title

Application number C 240218 ZMK (Park Avenue Brooklyn Rezoning) submitted by Park Avenue Rezoning Partners LLC, pursuant to Sections 197-c and 201 of the New York City Charter for an amendment of the Zoning Map, Section Nos. 12d & 13b: changing from an M1-1 District to an M1-4/R6-1 District; changing from an M1-2 District to an M1-4/R6-1 District; changing from an M1-1 District to an M1-4/R7D District; changing from an M1-2 District to an M1-4/R7D District; and establishing a Special Mixed Use District (MX-4), Borough of Brooklyn, Community District 3, Council District 33.

Summary

This land use application would amend the zoning map to rezone an area near Park Avenue in Brooklyn from manufacturing districts (M1-1 and M1-2) to mixed manufacturing-residential districts (M1-4/R6-1 and M1-4/R7D) and establish a Special Mixed Use District. The change affects property in Brooklyn Community District 3, Council District 33, and could allow new residential and commercial development alongside manufacturing uses.
