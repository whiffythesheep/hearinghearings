LU 0121-2026 (Passed Council: adopted)

Zoning map change for 862-868 Kent Avenue, Brooklyn

· Sponsor: Kevin C. Riley · Decided by Res 0660-2026 ↗ · View on Legistar ↗

Official title

Application number C 240283 ZMK (862-868 Kent Avenue) submitted by Kent Development LLC and 124 Taaffe LLC, pursuant to Sections 197-c and 201 of the New York City Charter for an amendment of the Zoning Map, Section Nos. 12d and 16c, changing from an M1-1 District to an M1-4/R6A District and establishing a Special Mixed Use District (MX-4), Borough of Brooklyn, Community District 3, Council District 33.

Summary

This application would change the zoning map to allow an M1-4/R6A district and a Special Mixed Use District (MX-4) at 862-868 Kent Avenue in Bedford-Stuyvesant, Brooklyn. Combined with a related text amendment, it would enable two new residential buildings with 74 units, 19 of them permanently income-restricted.
