Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to requiring the department of health and mental hygiene to make medication abortion available at no cost to a patient at all of its sexual health clinics

Summary

This bill would require the city's Department of Health and Mental Hygiene to offer free medication abortion at every health clinic it runs that offers sexual health services, not just the four clinics that currently do so. Currently DOHMH provides this medication at the Fort Greene, Jamaica, Morrisania and Central Harlem clinics, which together served 1,253 abortion patients in 2025. The law would take effect immediately upon passage.
