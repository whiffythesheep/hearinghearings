Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to requiring the department of health and mental hygiene to make medication abortion available at no cost to a patient at all of its sexual health clinics

Summary

This bill would require the Department of Health and Mental Hygiene to offer free FDA-approved medication abortion at all of its health clinics that provide sexual health services, not just those it currently designates as appropriate. Currently medication abortion is available at only four of DOHMH's eight Sexual Health Clinics: Fort Greene, Jamaica, Morrisania and Central Harlem. In 2025 those clinics served 1,253 abortion patients, a 24% increase from 2024.
