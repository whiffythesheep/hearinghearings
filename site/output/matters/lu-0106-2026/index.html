Land use

LU 0106-2026

Rezoning to allow housing at 1160 Pugsley Avenue, Bronx

Passed Council · Sponsor: Kevin C. Riley · View on Legistar ↗

In brief

This land use application would change the zoning map for a site on Pugsley Avenue in the Bronx from an R5 district to an R7A district with a C2-4 commercial overlay. The rezoning would allow an eight-story mixed-use building with ground-floor commercial space, including a grocery store, and 32 accessory parking spaces. It is linked to a related zoning text amendment establishing a Mandatory Inclusionary Housing area for the same site.

Official title

Application number C 250245 ZMX (1160 Pugsley Avenue Rezoning) submitted by 1160-1178 Pugsley Ave LLC, pursuant to Sections 197-c and 201 of the New York City Charter for an amendment of the Zoning Map, Section No. 4b, changing from an R5 District to an R7A District and establishing within the proposed R7A District a C2-4 District, Borough of the Bronx, Community District 9, Council District 18.
