Int 1067-2026 (In committee: awaiting a hearing)

Law to allow voluntary AI environmental disclosures in city contracts

· Sponsor: Susan Zhuang
+ 5 co-sponsors Farah N. Louis, Kevin C. Riley, Harvey D. Epstein, Shirley Aldebol, Public Advocate Jumaane Williams
· View on Legistar ↗

Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to voluntary environmental impact and energy use disclosures in city procurement of artificial intelligence tools

Summary

This bill would let city agencies request voluntary disclosures from companies bidding on city contracts for artificial intelligence tools about their efforts to address environmental impact, sustainability and energy use. The Mayor's Office of Contract Services would create a standard form for these disclosures, and not submitting one would not count against a bidder. It would take effect 120 days after becoming law.
