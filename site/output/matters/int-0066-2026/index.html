Int 0066-2026 (In committee: heard, awaiting a vote)

Law to allow new shared housing with private rooms

· Sponsor: Virginia Maloney
+ 14 co-sponsors Farah N. Louis, Lincoln Restler, Shaun Abreu, Oswald J. Feliz, Pierina Ana Sanchez, Shahana K. Hanif, Carl Wilson, Rita C. Joseph, Kamillah Hanks, Lynn C. Schulman, Elsie Encarnación, Chi A. Ossé, James F. Gennaro, Frank Morano
· View on Legistar ↗

Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, the New York city building code, and the New York city fire code, in relation to shared housing

Summary

This bill would let developers build "shared housing" starting January 1, 2027, where two or more private rooming units share a kitchen and bathroom in new or converted apartment buildings. It sets rules covering room size, occupancy limits, fire protection, cooking equipment, management and building design, adding a new appendix to the building code. Short-term rentals would not be allowed in these shared rooming units.
