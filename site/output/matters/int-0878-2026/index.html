Law to study filming's impact on local businesses

· Sponsor: Christopher Marte
+ 1 co-sponsor Farah N. Louis
· View on Legistar ↗

Official title

A Local Law in relation to requiring the department of small business services to conduct a study on the economic impact of film and television production on local businesses and develop a plan to mitigate lost revenue attributable to filming

Summary

This bill would require the Department of Small Business Services, with the Mayor's Office of Media and Entertainment, to study how film and television production affects local businesses' revenue. The agency would report its findings to the Mayor, the Council Speaker and the public within one year. The department would also develop a plan to help businesses recover revenue lost due to filming activity.
