Int 0717-2026 (In committee: heard, awaiting a vote)

Law to require DOB to remind owners about permit sign-offs

· Sponsor: Yusef Salaam
+ 11 co-sponsors Farah N. Louis, Selvena N. Brooks-Powers, Ty Hankerson, Harvey D. Epstein, Lynn C. Schulman, Sandy Nurse, Shirley Aldebol, Frank Morano, Vickie Paladino, Inna Vernikov, Public Advocate Jumaane Williams
· View on Legistar ↗

Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to requiring the department of buildings to inform property owners of permit sign-off requirements

Summary

This bill would require the Department of Buildings to email property owners a notice about permit sign-off requirements within seven days of a permit's issuance, and then every 90 days until sign-off is received. If a permit is close to expiring or has expired, DOB would have to send the notice every two weeks until sign-off. The notice must include the permit number, expiration date and consequences of not obtaining sign-off, such as reinstatement fees and effects on future permits or property sales.
