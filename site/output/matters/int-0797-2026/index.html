Law to report on jail voter registration data

Official title

A Local Law to amend the New York city charter, in relation to reporting on voter registration and voting in city jails

Summary

This bill would require the Department of Correction to issue an annual report on voter registration and voting activity in city jails, starting by January 31, 2027. The report would include the number of voter registration events held, the number of completed registration forms returned, and the number of absentee ballots distributed, broken down by facility and demographic factors like race, gender and disability status without personal identifying information. It follows findings that only a small share of eligible incarcerated voters, who numbered roughly 6,900 daily at Rikers, actually cast ballots in recent elections.
