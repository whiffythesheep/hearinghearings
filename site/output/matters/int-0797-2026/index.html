Official title

A Local Law to amend the New York city charter, in relation to reporting on voter registration and voting in city jails

Summary

This bill would require the Department of Correction to report annually, starting January 31, 2027, on voter registration and voting activities in city jails. The report must include the number of voter registration events, completed registration forms returned, and absentee ballots distributed, broken down by facility and demographic factors like race, age, gender and disability status. The report may not include personally identifiable information.
