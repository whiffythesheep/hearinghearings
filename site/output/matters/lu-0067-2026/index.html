Land use

LU 0067-2026

Zoning map change for Monitor Point, Greenpoint

Passed Council · Sponsor: Kevin C. Riley · View on Legistar ↗

In brief

This amends the zoning map to rezone land near Franklin Street and Quay Street in Greenpoint, Brooklyn from manufacturing (M3-1) and lower-density residential (R6) districts to higher-density R8 and R6 districts, including a new commercial overlay. It is one of several linked land use actions that would allow a mixed-use development with about 1,150 homes, including 300 income-restricted units, plus commercial and community space. The Council held a public hearing on May 27, 2026 and its Land Use Committee acted on June 25, 2026.

Official title

Application number C 260105 ZMK (Monitor Point) submitted by GoQuay LLC and the NYS Metropolitan Transportation Authority, pursuant to Sections 197-c and 201 of the New York City Charter for an amendment of the Zoning Map, Section No. 12c: changing from an M3-1 District to an R6 District; changing from an R6 District to an R8 District; changing from an M3-1 District to an R8 District; and establishing within a portion of the proposed R8 District a C2-4 District, Borough of Brooklyn, Community District 1, Council District 33.
