Official title

Resolution declaring August 2 annually as James Baldwin Day in the City of New York to honor his legacy as a groundbreaking essayist, novelist, playwright, poet, and civil rights activist, who fought racial and sexual discrimination with candor, sensitivity, and lasting influence

Summary

This resolution would designate August 2, the birthday of writer and civil rights activist James Baldwin, as James Baldwin Day in New York City every year. It honors Baldwin, born in Harlem in 1924, for his work as an essayist, novelist, playwright and poet who wrote about racial and sexual discrimination.
