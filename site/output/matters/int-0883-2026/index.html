Int 0883-2026 (In committee: heard, awaiting a vote)

Law to create a sidewalk cafe cleanliness office

· Sponsor: Justin E. Sanchez
+ 2 co-sponsors Farah N. Louis, Kevin C. Riley
· View on Legistar ↗

Official title

A Local Law to amend the New York city charter, in relation to establishing an office of sidewalk and roadway cafe sanitation within the department of sanitation

Summary

This bill would require the Department of Sanitation to set up an Office of Sidewalk and Roadway Cafe Sanitation to handle 311 complaints about dirty sidewalk and roadway cafes. The office would track cafes with repeated violations or complaints, share those lists with the Department of Transportation, do outreach to cafe operators and issue an annual public report. It would take effect 180 days after becoming law.
