Law to require a battery disposal plan for e-bikes and scooters

· Sponsor: Sandy Nurse
+ 15 co-sponsors Farah N. Louis, Althea V. Stevens, Rita C. Joseph, Christopher Marte, Gale A. Brewer, Crystal Hudson, Selvena N. Brooks-Powers, Shahana K. Hanif, Sandra Ung, Jennifer Gutiérrez, Yusef Salaam, Harvey D. Epstein, Shirley Aldebol, Frank Morano, Public Advocate Jumaane Williams
· View on Legistar ↗

Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to requiring the department of sanitation to develop a plan for ensuring proper disposal of rechargeable batteries used for powered mobility devices

Summary

This bill would require the Department of Sanitation to create a plan for the proper disposal of rechargeable batteries used in motorized bicycles, scooters and similar mobility devices. The plan must include at least two collection sites per borough open seven days a week, battery collection at disposal events, coordination with retailers on in-store collection, and a public education campaign in the city's designated languages. The law would take effect immediately upon passage.
