Int 0296-2026 (Passed Council: awaiting the Mayor)

Law to improve data on gender-based violence

· Sponsor: Farah N. Louis
+ 14 co-sponsors Amanda C. Farías, Ty Hankerson, Chris Banks, Sandy Nurse, Linda Lee, Tiffany L. Cabán, Shirley Aldebol, Selvena N. Brooks-Powers, Mercedes Narcisse, Harvey D. Epstein, Alexa Avilés, Crystal Hudson, Kevin C. Riley, Public Advocate Jumaane Williams
· View on Legistar ↗

Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to increasing access to data regarding gender-based violence

Summary

This bill would require the Mayor's Office to End Gender-Based Violence, with the health department, to review existing data on gender-based violence and report recommendations for better data collection within one year. It would also require an outreach program surveying healthcare providers' screening practices, a public data dashboard combining city and state information, and reporting by the Department of Homeless Services and Administration for Children's Services on their own gender-based violence screenings.
