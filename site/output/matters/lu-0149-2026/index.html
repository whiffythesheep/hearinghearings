Zoning text change for 108-05 68th Road, Forest Hills

· Sponsor: Kevin C. Riley · View on Legistar ↗

Official title

Application number N 260235 ZRQ (108-05 68th Road Rezoning) submitted by All My Children Daycare and Nursery School, pursuant to Section 201 of the New York City Charter, for an amendment of the Zoning Resolution of the City of New York, amending APPENDIX F for the purpose of establishing a Mandatory Inclusionary Housing Area, Borough of Queens, Community District 6, Council District 29.

Summary

This application would establish a Mandatory Inclusionary Housing area for a site at 108-05 68th Road in Forest Hills, Queens, paired with a related zoning map change. It would help enable a seven-story mixed-use building with about 33 apartments, including 8 income-restricted units, and 11,500 square feet of community facility space. The Council held a public hearing on October 6, 2026.
