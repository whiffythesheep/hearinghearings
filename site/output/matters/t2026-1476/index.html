Official title

Oversight - Finalizing the City’s Draft 2026-2036 Solid Waste Management Plan.

Summary

The Council's Sanitation Committee held an oversight hearing on the Department of Sanitation's draft 2026-2036 Solid Waste Management Plan, which sets goals for waste reduction, recycling and composting through 2036. The hearing also covered a bill requiring city agencies to recycle organic waste and a resolution urging Albany to require composting at SUNY and CUNY facilities. The draft plan aims to raise waste diversion from about 18.5 percent to as much as 30.5 percent by 2036.
