LU 0145-2026 (Passed Council: adopted)

Zoning text change for affordable housing, Sapphire Street, Queens

· Sponsor: Kevin C. Riley · Decided by Res 0714-2026 ↗ · View on Legistar ↗

Official title

Application number N 250330 ZRQ (135-27 Sapphire Street Rezoning) submitted by 135 Sapphire LLC, pursuant to Section 201 of the New York City Charter, for an amendment of the Zoning Resolution of the City of New York, amending APPENDIX F for the purpose of establishing a Mandatory Inclusionary Housing Area, Borough of Queens, Community District 10, Council District 32.

Summary

This application establishes a Mandatory Inclusionary Housing area at 135-27 Sapphire Street in Queens Community District 10, working alongside a related rezoning from R4 to R6A. Together the actions would allow a new six-story residential building with about 186,380 square feet of floor area and roughly 268 units, of which about 67 would be permanently income-restricted. The City Council approved the measure on October 22, 2026.
