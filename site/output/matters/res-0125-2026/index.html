Res 0125-2026

Resolution urging Albany to require SUNY and CUNY composting

In committee · Sponsors: Sandy Nurse, Eric Dinowitz, Farah N. Louis, Althea V. Stevens, Rita C. Joseph, Harvey D. Epstein, Jennifer Gutiérrez, Yusef Salaam, Crystal Hudson · View on Legistar ↗

In brief

This resolution calls on the New York State Legislature to pass, and the Governor to sign, S.5713/A.3249, which would require SUNY and CUNY institutions to separate compostable waste in dormitories, dining facilities and maintenance operations. The bill would also require SUNY and CUNY trustees to annually report on their composting programs. It aims to close gaps left by existing state and City organic waste recycling laws that exclude many college facilities.

Official title

Resolution calling on the New York State Legislature to pass, and the Governor to sign, S.2285, to require the establishment of a composting program at dormitories, dining facilities, and other facilities owned, occupied, or operated by the State University of New York, the City University of New York, and institutions subject to their jurisdiction
