Int 0030-2026 (In committee: heard, awaiting a vote)

Law to require on-street trash bins at city buildings

· Sponsor: Shaun Abreu
+ 2 co-sponsors Farah N. Louis, Harvey D. Epstein
· View on Legistar ↗

Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to stationary on-street containers for the storage and collection of waste from buildings owned by the city

Summary

This bill would direct the Department of Sanitation, working with the Department of Citywide Administrative Services and the Department of Education, to create a program requiring city-owned non-residential buildings to dispose of waste using stationary on-street containers rather than loose bags. Buildings with off-street collection, such as those with loading docks, would be exempt. All covered city buildings would need to comply by June 1, 2030, and the Department would have to report on implementation plans and costs.
