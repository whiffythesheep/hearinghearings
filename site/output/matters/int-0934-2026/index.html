Int 0934-2026 (In committee: heard, awaiting a vote)

Law to add higher tax breaks for lower-income senior homeowners

· Sponsor: Simcha Felder
+ 9 co-sponsors Phil Wong, Farah N. Louis, Lynn C. Schulman, Lincoln Restler, Shirley Aldebol, Virginia Maloney, Selvena N. Brooks-Powers, Carl Wilson, Frank Morano
· View on Legistar ↗

Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to the creation of additional annual income categories and corresponding exemption percentages for certain households eligible for the senior citizens homeowners’ exemption

Summary

This bill would let seniors with the lowest incomes get a bigger property tax exemption under the Senior Citizen Homeowners' Exemption. Starting July 1, 2026, homeowners earning less than $47,000 could get a 65 percent exemption, with 60 percent for those earning $47,000-$48,000 and 55 percent for those earning $48,000-$49,000, up from the current 50 percent cap. The change builds on a recent state law allowing cities to expand this tax relief.
