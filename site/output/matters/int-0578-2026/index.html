Introduction

Int 0578-2026

Law to expand and add rules for the industrial zone boundary board

In committee · Sponsors: Jennifer Gutiérrez, Farah N. Louis, Alexa Avilés · View on Legistar ↗

In brief

This bill would add three members to the Industrial Business Zone Boundary Commission, two appointed by the Council Speaker and one by the Public Advocate, and require a two-thirds vote to amend or repeal existing zone boundaries. It would bar commission members from having financial ties to real estate or land use firms, ban registered lobbyists from serving, and require annual financial disclosures. The commission would also have to notify the Council within five business days of any approved boundary change and post meeting materials online, with the law taking effect 120 days after enactment.

Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to reforming the industrial business zone boundary commission
