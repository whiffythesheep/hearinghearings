Int 0685-2026

Law to create a city Office of Insurance Accountability

Law or adopted · Sponsors: Julie Menin, Gale A. Brewer, Lincoln Restler, Farah N. Louis, Shirley Aldebol, Lynn C. Schulman, Chris Banks, Linda Lee, Shanel Thomas-Henry, Harvey D. Epstein, Carl Wilson, Althea V. Stevens, Justin E. Sanchez, Mercedes Narcisse, Eric Dinowitz, Virginia Maloney, Tiffany L. Cabán, Joann Ariola · View on Legistar ↗

In brief

This bill would establish an Office of Insurance Accountability within the Department of Consumer and Worker Protection to give consumers guidance on choosing insurance and track legal actions against insurers for deceptive practices. The office would conduct annual studies on insurance costs, issue reports with legislative and policy recommendations, and run a unit led by an insurance accountability advocate to help consumers resolve insurance issues. It covers all insurance types except health insurance and would take effect 240 days after becoming law.
