Int 1027-2026 (Law: Local Law 156 of 2026)

Law to co-name seven streets and public places

· Enacted October 10, 2026 · Sponsor: Tiffany L. Cabán
+ 6 co-sponsors David M. Carr, Carmen N. De La Rosa, Crystal Hudson, Vickie Paladino, Nantasha M. Williams, Carl Wilson
· View on Legistar ↗

Official title

A Local Law in relation to the naming of 7 thoroughfares and public places, Champion’s Plaza, Borough of Manhattan, Apostle Dr. Carolyn Younger-Nolan Way, Borough of Queens, SGT. Terrance McAvoy Way, Borough of Staten Island, Leonardo A. Dilone Figueredo Way, Borough of Manhattan, Little Panama, Borough of Brooklyn, Millie Rigoli Way, Borough of Queens, Colosseum Way, Borough of Queens and the repeal of sections 12, 23, 51, 74 and 94 of local law number 125 for the year 2026 and section 5 of local law number 26 for the year 2020

Summary

This bill would add seven honorary street co-names across Manhattan, Queens, Staten Island and Brooklyn, including Champion's Plaza honoring the 2026 NBA champion Knicks and Little Panama recognizing Brooklyn's Panamanian community, plus several honoring individuals who died between 2012 and 2024. It also repeals five sections of a 2026 local law and one section of a 2020 local law related to prior street namings.
