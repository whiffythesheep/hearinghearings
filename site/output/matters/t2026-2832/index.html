T2026-2832 (In committee: awaiting a hearing)

Law to toughen penalties for unsafe e-bike batteries

· Sponsor: James F. Gennaro · View on Legistar ↗

Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York and the New York city fire code, in relation to penalties for the possession, sale, lease, or rental of unsafe powered bicycles, powered mobility devices, and storage batteries and the assembly or sale of second-use lithium-ion batteries

Summary

This bill would make it illegal to store, handle, use or possess powered bicycles, mobility devices or their batteries unless they meet city safety standards. Violations would carry civil penalties up to $1,000 and a misdemeanor charge punishable by up to one year in prison. It also sets penalties for assembling or selling reused lithium-ion batteries.
