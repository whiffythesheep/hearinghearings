Int 0078-2026 (In committee: heard, awaiting a vote)

Law to require delivery data tracking from delivery services

· Sponsor: Gale A. Brewer
+ 13 co-sponsors Farah N. Louis, Tiffany L. Cabán, Lincoln Restler, Shanel Thomas-Henry, Althea V. Stevens, Lynn C. Schulman, Shahana K. Hanif, Susan Zhuang, Sandy Nurse, Alexa Avilés, Elsie Encarnación, Vickie Paladino, Public Advocate Jumaane Williams
· View on Legistar ↗

Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to the retention of delivery data

Summary

This bill would require licensed delivery services to submit detailed trip data to the Department of Consumer and Worker Protection, including delivery worker identification, timing of orders and pickups, drop-off locations, GPS route data and vehicle type used. The law would take effect alongside a related bill (Introduction 1448) on licensing delivery services.
