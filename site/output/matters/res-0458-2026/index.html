Official title

Resolution calling on the Governor to sign S.6487C/A.7033D to empower the New York State Council on the Arts to designate Little Yemen as a cultural district

Summary

This resolution calls on the Governor to sign state legislation (S.6487C/A.7033D) authorizing the New York State Council on the Arts to designate the Bronx neighborhood known as Little Yemen as a cultural district. The district's boundaries would be White Plains Road to the west, Bronxdale Avenue to the east and north, and Van Nest Avenue to the south. The designation would make cultural institutions within the district eligible for state grants and technical assistance.
