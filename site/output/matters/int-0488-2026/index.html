Law requiring yearly reports on veteran preference in Mitchell-Lama housing

· Sponsor: Inna Vernikov
+ 15 co-sponsors Farah N. Louis, Gale A. Brewer, Linda Lee, Yusef Salaam, Phil Wong, Mercedes Narcisse, Eric Dinowitz, Chris Banks, Kamillah Hanks, Kevin C. Riley, Lynn C. Schulman, James F. Gennaro, Frank Morano, Vickie Paladino, David M. Carr

Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to requiring the department of housing preservation and development to submit reports on veteran preference in Mitchell-Lama developments

Summary

This bill would require the Department of Housing Preservation and Development to submit an annual report on veteran preference in city-run Mitchell-Lama developments. The report must detail the number of veterans or surviving spouses who applied and were selected for occupancy, the documentation used to verify veteran status, and the safeguards in place to confirm eligibility. Reports would be due by September 1 of each year to the mayor, the Council speaker and the public advocate.
