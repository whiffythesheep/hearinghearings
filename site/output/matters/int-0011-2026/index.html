Int 0011-2026

Law to raise fines for truck and bus idling

In brief

This bill would raise civil penalties for illegal truck and bus idling in New York City, with first violations rising to $1,000-$2,000 and penalties increasing further for repeat violations within three years. It would also align the definitions of bus and truck with state traffic law for clearer enforcement. The law would take effect 120 days after being enacted.

Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to increasing civil penalties for idling infractions
