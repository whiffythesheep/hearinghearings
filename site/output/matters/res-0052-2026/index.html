Resolution

Res 0052-2026

Resolution urging Albany to expand Fair Fares to commuter rail

In committee · Sponsors: Selvena N. Brooks-Powers, Farah N. Louis, Harvey D. Epstein · View on Legistar ↗

In brief

This resolution calls on the State Legislature and Governor to pass legislation requiring a 50 percent fare discount for Fair Fares-eligible New Yorkers and those earning up to 200 percent of the federal poverty level on the Long Island Rail Road, Metro-North, paratransit and city subways and buses. It would also require the MTA, working with the City, to run a public outreach campaign to boost Fair Fares enrollment. Fair Fares currently serves about 379,000 enrollees, while an estimated 450,000 more New Yorkers could become eligible under the expanded income threshold.

Official title

Resolution calling on the New York State Legislature to pass, and the New York State Governor to sign, S.3887A/A.7316A, requiring that any individual who is eligible for the Fair Fares NYC program and any person whose income is 200 percent of the federal poverty level receive a 50 percent discount on trips using the Long Island Rail Road, paratransit services, New York City Transit subways or buses, and the Metro-North Railroad
