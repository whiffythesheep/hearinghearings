Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to requiring the department of education to distribute information about the importance of dental care

Summary

This bill would require the Department of Education, in consultation with the Department of Health and Mental Hygiene, to develop materials on dental care and fluoride and distribute them annually to every student and parent in New York City public schools. The materials must use plain, age-appropriate language and be available in English and the nine most common home languages of students, plus additional languages as needed.
