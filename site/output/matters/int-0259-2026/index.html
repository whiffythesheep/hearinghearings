Law to require dental health materials for students and parents

Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to requiring the department of education to distribute information about the importance of dental care

Summary

This bill would require the Department of Education to work with the Department of Health and Mental Hygiene to create materials on dental care, oral hygiene and fluoride. Schools would have to distribute these materials annually to every student and parent, in English and the nine most common home languages. The law would take effect immediately.
