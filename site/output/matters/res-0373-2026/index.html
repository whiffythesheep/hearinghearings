Official title

Resolution calling on the New York State Legislature to pass, and the Governor to sign, A.1499/S.1179, which would provide long term care benefits for eligible residents

Summary

This resolution calls on the New York State Legislature to pass, and the Governor to sign, bills A.1499/S.1179, which would create a long term care trust program funded by payroll contributions. Eligible residents who paid into the program and need help with at least two daily living activities could receive benefits. The resolution cites about 250,000 New York City residents enrolled in Managed Long Term Care Plans as of December 2022.
