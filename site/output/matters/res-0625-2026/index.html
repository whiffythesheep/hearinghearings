Resolution declaring October Reggaeton Heritage Month in New York City

· Sponsor: Farah N. Louis

Official title

Resolution declaring October as Reggaeton Heritage Month in the City of New York to honor the legacy of reggaeton as a prominent expression of Afro-Caribbean and Latin American identity and to recognize New York’s pivotal role in facilitating the blend of cultures that is at the heart of this grassroots musical genre

Summary

This resolution would declare October as Reggaeton Heritage Month in New York City to honor reggaeton's roots in Afro-Caribbean and Latin American culture. It credits New York City, particularly Brooklyn, with helping blend dancehall, reggae and hip-hop into the genre starting in the mid-1980s. The resolution does not carry the force of law but recognizes the city's role in reggaeton's development.
