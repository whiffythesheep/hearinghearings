Resolution

Res 0276-2026

Resolution urging Congress to fund guaranteed income programs

In committee · Sponsors: Nantasha M. Williams, Chi A. Ossé, Selvena N. Brooks-Powers, Farah N. Louis, Justin E. Sanchez, Althea V. Stevens, Crystal Hudson · View on Legistar ↗

In brief

This resolution calls on Congress to pass, and the President to sign, legislation creating targeted, means-tested guaranteed income programs for low-income individuals, young adults and foster youth. It references pending federal bills including a national guaranteed income pilot, a $500 monthly payment for young adults aged 18-24, and $1,000 monthly payments for foster youth up to age 27. The Council argues these programs would help address poverty among New Yorkers, including nearly 6,500 children in foster care as of September 2024.
