Introduction

Int 0820-2026

Law to create a child care worker training grant program

In committee · Sponsors: Jennifer Gutiérrez, Farah N. Louis, Julie Won, Lincoln Restler, Virginia Maloney, Tiffany L. Cabán, Alexa Avilés, Mercedes Narcisse, Carl Wilson · View on Legistar ↗

In brief

This bill would require a city agency to run a program giving loans or grants to people without child care experience so they can complete required education and certification to work in child care. Participants must agree to work in child care in New York City for at least 5 years afterward, and the program must also provide child care for participants during their training and conduct outreach to the public and high school students. The agency must begin accepting applications within 6 months of the law taking effect, and the law would take effect one year after enactment.

Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to establishing a child care workforce grant and services program
