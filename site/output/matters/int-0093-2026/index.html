Proposed law

Int 0093-2026

Law to expand bicycle parking stations citywide

Law · Sponsors: Gale A. Brewer, Farah N. Louis, Shahana K. Hanif, Christopher Marte, Harvey D. Epstein, Tiffany L. Cabán, Alexa Avilés, Shirley Aldebol, Julie Won, Crystal Hudson, Kamillah Hanks, Amanda C. Farías, Sandy Nurse, Carl Wilson, Shaun Abreu · View on Legistar ↗

In brief

This bill would require the Department of Transportation to install at least 5,000 bicycle parking stations over five years, at a rate of at least 1,000 per year, including at least 2,000 on commercial blocks. DOT would need to consider community input, delivery worker data and proximity to bike infrastructure when choosing locations, and post a public map of all stations. By January 1, 2032, DOT must report to the Mayor and Council on the program's cost and effectiveness.

Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to the bicycle parking station program
