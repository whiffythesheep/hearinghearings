LU 0134-2026 (Passed Council: adopted)

Zoning text change for Park Avenue rezoning, Bedford-Stuyvesant

· Sponsor: Kevin C. Riley · Decided by Res 0682-2026 ↗ · View on Legistar ↗

Official title

Application number N 240219 ZRK (Park Avenue Brooklyn Rezoning) submitted by Park Avenue Rezoning Partners LLC, pursuant to Section 201 of the New York City Charter, for an amendment of the Zoning Resolution of the City of New York, amending APPENDIX F for the purpose of establishing a Mandatory Inclusionary Housing Area, Borough of Brooklyn, Community District 3, Council District 33.

Summary

This application would amend the Zoning Resolution to establish a Mandatory Inclusionary Housing area along Park Avenue in Bedford-Stuyvesant, Brooklyn, in conjunction with a related zoning map change. Together the actions would allow ten new buildings of five to 11 stories with 391 housing units, 101 of them permanently income-restricted, plus commercial and school space. The Council approved the plan with modifications on October 8, 2026.
