Introduction

Int 0822-2026

Law to co-name streets for Thierry Henry and Pelé

Law or adopted · Sponsors: Virginia Maloney, Shanel Thomas-Henry, Ty Hankerson, Selvena N. Brooks-Powers, Julie Won, Farah N. Louis · View on Legistar ↗

In brief

This bill would co-name the intersection of West 50th Street and 6th Avenue in Manhattan as Thierry Henry Way and the intersection of Shea Road and Meridian Road in Queens as Pelé Way. It would take effect immediately and expire on November 1, 2026.
