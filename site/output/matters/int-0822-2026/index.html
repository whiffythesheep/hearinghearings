Int 0822-2026 (Law: Local Law 108 of 2026)

Law to co-name streets for Thierry Henry and Pelé

· Enacted June 13, 2026 · Sponsor: Virginia Maloney
+ 5 co-sponsors Shanel Thomas-Henry, Ty Hankerson, Selvena N. Brooks-Powers, Julie Won, Farah N. Louis
· View on Legistar ↗

Official title

A Local Law in relation to the naming of two thoroughfares and public places, Thierry Henry Way, Borough of Manhattan and Pel? Way, Borough of Queens, and the repeal of this local law upon the expiration thereof

Summary

This bill would co-name the intersection of West 50th Street and 6th Avenue in Manhattan as Thierry Henry Way and the intersection of Shea Road and Meridian Road in Queens as Pelé Way. It would take effect immediately and expire on November 1, 2026.
