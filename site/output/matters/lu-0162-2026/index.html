Application for housing project at 63 Exterior Street, Bronx

· Sponsor: Kevin C. Riley · View on Legistar ↗

Official title

Application number C 260193 HAX (110 East 138th Street/63 Exterior Street) submitted by the New York City Department of Housing Preservation and Development (HPD), pursuant to Article 16 of the General Municipal Law of New York State for the designation of an Urban Development Action Area and an Urban Development Action Area Project, for property located at 63 Exterior Street (Block 2323, Lots 28 and 29) and 110 East 138th Street (Block 2323, Lot 43), and pursuant to Section 197-c of the New York City Charter for the disposition of such property to a developer to be selected by HPD, for property located at 63 Exterior Street (Block 2323, Lots 28 and 29), Borough of the Bronx, Community District 1, Council District 8.

Summary

The city Department of Housing Preservation and Development seeks an Urban Development Action Area designation for property at 63 Exterior Street and 110 East 138th Street in the Bronx. The action would allow the city to dispose of the Exterior Street property to a developer chosen by HPD for a housing project. The site is in Community District 1 and Council District 8.
