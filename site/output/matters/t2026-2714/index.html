Law to require rip current warning signs at city beaches

+ 1 co-sponsor Farah N. Louis
· View on Legistar ↗

Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to digital signage at public beaches to provide public safety information about rip currents and other water safety hazards

Summary

This bill would require the Parks Department to install digital signs at the entrances of all city beaches displaying audio and visual warnings about rip currents, weather hazards and other water safety threats. The signs would also explain what swimmers should do if caught in a rip current and how to get help, in the city's designated citywide languages. The law would take effect 180 days after being enacted.
