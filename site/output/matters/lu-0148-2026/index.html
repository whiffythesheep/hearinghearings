LU 0148-2026 (Reported from Committee)

Zoning map change for 108-05 68th Road, Forest Hills

· Sponsor: Kevin C. Riley · View on Legistar ↗

Official title

Application number C 260234 ZMQ (108-05 68th Road Rezoning) submitted by All My Children Daycare and Nursery School, pursuant to Sections 197-c and 201 of the New York City Charter for an amendment of the Zoning Map, Section No. 14a, changing from an R1-2A District to an R7A District, Borough of Queens, Community District 6, Council District 29.

Summary

This application would change the zoning map for a site at 108-05 68th Road in Forest Hills, Queens, from R1-2A to R7A. It would allow a seven-story mixed-use building with about 38,900 square feet of floor area, including 33 apartments (eight income-restricted) and 11,500 square feet of community facility space.
