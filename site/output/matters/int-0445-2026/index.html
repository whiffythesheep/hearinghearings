Int 0445-2026 (In committee: heard, awaiting a vote)

Law to expand small business training for NYCHA residents

· Sponsor: Althea V. Stevens
+ 4 co-sponsors Chris Banks, Lynn C. Schulman, Kevin C. Riley, Frank Morano
· View on Legistar ↗

Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to requiring the department of small business services to expand the business pathways program

Summary

This bill would require the Department of Small Business Services, working with NYCHA's Office of Resident Economic Empowerment and Sustainability, to expand its Business Pathways program for public housing residents beyond catering and childcare to include creative fields, retail and cosmetology. The free program would offer business education, mentorship, licensing help, operational guidance, safety training funding and help securing commercial space. It would take effect 120 days after becoming law.
