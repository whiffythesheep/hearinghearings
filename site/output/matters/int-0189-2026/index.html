Int 0189-2026 (In committee: heard, awaiting a vote)

Law to require photos of health code violations at restaurants

· Sponsor: Amanda C. Farías
+ 11 co-sponsors Farah N. Louis, Julie Won, Althea V. Stevens, Shekar Krishnan, Crystal Hudson, Shahana K. Hanif, Sandy Nurse, Oswald J. Feliz, Yusef Salaam, Inna Vernikov, Joann Ariola
· View on Legistar ↗

Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to requiring that notices of violation issued by the department of health and mental hygiene during inspections of food service establishments include photographs or video recordings of each violation cited in such notice

Summary

This bill would require city health inspectors to include photographs or video documenting each violation when a food service establishment scores 14 or more points during a sanitary inspection. The documentation would be kept on file and available as evidence in any hearing over the violation. The law would take effect 180 days after being enacted.
