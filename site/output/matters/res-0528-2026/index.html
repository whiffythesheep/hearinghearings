Resolution

Res 0528-2026

Resolution urging Albany to mandate Black history education

Law or adopted · Sponsors: Rita C. Joseph, Amanda C. Farías, Carl Wilson, Farah N. Louis, Lincoln Restler · View on Legistar ↗

In brief

This resolution calls on the New York State Legislature to pass, and the Governor to sign, legislation amending the Education Law to require public schools to teach the history, contributions and experiences of people of African descent. It notes that current State law mandates instruction on topics like slavery and the Holocaust but has no comparable requirement for Black history. It cites a December 2025 State law creating a similar survey and advisory committee for Asian American, Native Hawaiian and Pacific Islander history as a model.

Official title

Resolution calling on the New York State Legislature to introduce and pass, and the Governor to sign, legislation to amend the Education Law, in relation to requiring instruction on the history, contributions, and experiences of people of African descent in public schools
