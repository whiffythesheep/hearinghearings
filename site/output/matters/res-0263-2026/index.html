Official title

Resolution calling on the Federal Government to mandate basic training in addiction treatment as a requirement for medical schools that receive federal funding

Summary

This resolution calls on the federal government to mandate that medical schools receiving federal funding provide basic training in addiction treatment. It cites rising overdose deaths nationally and in New York City, including roughly 3,000 deaths in the 12 months ending August 2022, as justification for ensuring doctors can recognize and treat substance use disorders.
