Law to ban giving live fish as prizes

Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to banning the use of live fish as prizes

Summary

This bill would ban giving live fish as prizes in games, drawings, contests or similar promotions in New York City, with an exception for food or retail establishments selling live fish for consumption. Violators would face civil penalties of $25 per violation, and event sponsors who allow such violations at permitted street events could face penalties up to $2,000 per event. The city would also be required to post educational materials on the harms of disposing of live fish in waterways and best practices for caring for pet fish.
