Law to require bike and scooter share apps to show safety rules

· Sponsor: Shahana K. Hanif
+ 9 co-sponsors Selvena N. Brooks-Powers, Gale A. Brewer, Lincoln Restler, Farah N. Louis, Crystal Hudson, Shaun Abreu, Lynn C. Schulman, Shanel Thomas-Henry, Shirley Aldebol
· View on Legistar ↗

Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to requiring micromobility share system operators to display rules of the road for safe operation

Summary

This bill would require operators of shared bike and scooter systems to display traffic safety rules to users through their apps and at physical docking stations. Users would have to review the rules at least once a year before unlocking a device, with no fee charged for that time, and stations must display the rules within one year of the law taking effect. The rules must cover topics like yielding to pedestrians, obeying traffic signals, riding direction, sidewalk use, bell use, headphone use and safe speeds.
