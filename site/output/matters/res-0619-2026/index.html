Resolution urging Albany to expand design-build construction rules

· Sponsor: Eric Dinowitz
+ 5 co-sponsors Lincoln Restler, Justin E. Sanchez, Kamillah Hanks, Frank Morano, Public Advocate Jumaane Williams
· View on Legistar ↗

Official title

Resolution calling on the New York State Legislature and Governor to amend the New York City Public Works Investment Act to lower the monetary threshold to utilize design-build in construction projects.

Summary

This resolution calls on the New York State Legislature and Governor to amend the New York City Public Works Investment Act to lower the dollar threshold required for city agencies to use design-build contracts. Design-build currently applies mainly to projects costing $10 million or more, or $1.2 million for agencies like Parks and NYCHA. Lowering the threshold would let more projects, such as installing prefabricated park toilets, use the faster design-build process.
