T2026-2587

Law to require food delivery apps to flag unsafe worker driving

In committee · Sponsors: Lincoln Restler, Virginia Maloney · View on Legistar ↗

In brief

This bill would require third-party food delivery services to use tracking technology that detects when a delivery worker on an e-bike or moped speeds, rides the wrong way or rides on the sidewalk, and to notify the worker in real time. Workers who get three such notifications within seven days could not be assigned more deliveries until they complete a road safety course approved by the Department of Transportation. Delivery services would also have to submit quarterly reports on these notifications. It would take effect 180 days after becoming law.

Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to requiring third-party delivery services to track and notify certain food delivery workers of certain unsafe traffic behaviors
