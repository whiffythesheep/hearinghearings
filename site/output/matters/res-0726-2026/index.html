Resolution

Res 0726-2026 (Passed Council: adopted)

Resolution resetting Fiscal 2027 property tax rates and levy

· Sponsor: Linda Lee · View on Legistar ↗

Official title

Resolution amending and restating the resolution to provide the amounts necessary for the support of the government of the City of New York and the counties therein and for the payment of indebtedness thereof, for the Fiscal Year beginning on July 1, 2026 and ending on June 30, 2027, by the levy of taxes on the real property in the City of New York, in accordance with the provisions of the constitution of the State of New York, the Real Property Law and the Charter of the City of New York.

Summary

This resolution amends and restates the Council's prior resolution fixing real property tax rates for Fiscal Year 2027, following a state law change allowing the city to adjust the cap on how much any property tax class's share can grow, now set at 0.3 percent. It sets the total tax levy at $39,885,078,591 to raise $37,190,000,000 net toward the $125,841,596,044 Fiscal 2027 budget, and fixes new tax rates by property class, including 0.19972 for one-, two- and three-family homes. The Council plans to adopt this alongside related base proportion resolutions on November 12, 2026.
