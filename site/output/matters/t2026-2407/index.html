Hearing on New York's new graduation framework

· View on Legistar ↗

Official title

Oversight - Portrait of a Graduate.

Summary

The Council's Education Committee is examining the state's shift from Regents exam requirements to the new Portrait of a Graduate framework, which will phase out standalone diploma exams and move to one diploma type by 2027-28. The hearing will look at how the city's Department of Education is preparing for the change, which affects over 400 high schools and 300,000 students, and at concerns about guidance gaps and equity for Black, Latino and low-income students. Full statewide implementation, including new transcripts, is planned to begin in fall 2029.
