Int 0077-2026 (In committee: heard, awaiting a vote)

Law to make pedicab fare timers more visible

· Sponsor: Gale A. Brewer
+ 3 co-sponsors Farah N. Louis, Carl Wilson, Frank Morano
· View on Legistar ↗

Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to the visibility of the ride timer in pedicabs

Summary

This bill would require pedicabs to mount the ride timer on the rear of the bicycle seat, within clear view of all passengers. It amends existing pedicab safety and signage rules in the city's administrative code. The change aims to reduce overcharging and unclear fare disputes, one of the most common pedicab-related complaints to 311.
