Hearing on court delays and the jail population

· View on Legistar ↗

Official title

Oversight - Improving Court Operations to Reduce the Jail Population.

Summary

The Council's Committee on Criminal Justice will examine how slow criminal case processing contributes to New York City's jail population and complicates the planned closure of Rikers Island by August 31, 2027. The hearing will review efforts by the courts, District Attorneys, NYPD and other agencies to speed up cases, including discovery compliance and scheduling reforms, and will hear testimony from officials at the Office of Court Administration, NYPD, the Mayor's Office of Criminal Justice and District Attorneys' offices. Officials project the jail population could reach about 8,800 by June 2027 without changes, far exceeding the planned borough-based jails' capacity of about 4,160.
