Official title

Resolution opposing the federally proposed rule change titled, “Reducing Federal Burden for Head Start Programs” and calling on the Federal Government to prioritize protecting and growing access to high-quality education for young children

Summary

This resolution opposes a federal proposal, released August 6 2026, that would roll back Head Start regulations by cutting staff-to-child ratio rules, dropping staff qualification requirements and mandating English-only instruction. It calls on the federal government to instead prioritize protecting and expanding access to high-quality early education. In New York State, Head Start serves about 43,300 children, and the English-only rule could affect many of the state's 554,000 multilingual young children.
