Law requiring NYPD security for threatened city officials

· Sponsor: Crystal Hudson
+ 1 co-sponsor Nantasha M. Williams
· View on Legistar ↗

Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to requiring the police department to provide security details to elected and appointed city officials that receive threats

Summary

This bill would require the NYPD to provide a personal security detail to any elected or appointed city official who receives a credible threat to their safety. The size, scope and duration of the detail would be decided by the police commissioner, and the official could decline the protection. The law would take effect immediately upon passage.
