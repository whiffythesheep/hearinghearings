Int 0202-2026 (In committee: heard, awaiting a vote)

Law to require faster traffic safety fixes near schools

· Sponsor: Jennifer Gutiérrez
+ 11 co-sponsors Farah N. Louis, Shahana K. Hanif, Phil Wong, Mercedes Narcisse, Kayla Santosuosso, Justin E. Sanchez, Susan Zhuang, Harvey D. Epstein, Chris Banks, Kamillah Hanks, Sandra Ung
· View on Legistar ↗

Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to the time permitted for the installation of a traffic calming device or traffic control device on any street adjacent to a school

Summary

This bill would require the Department of Transportation to complete installation of any traffic calming or traffic control device found necessary near a school within 60 days of its traffic study determination. The requirement would not apply to devices installed as part of a major transportation project. The bill takes effect immediately upon enactment.
