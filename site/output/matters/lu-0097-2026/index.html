LU 0097-2026 (Passed Council: adopted)

Zoning text change for 50-20 108th Street, Corona

· Sponsor: Kevin C. Riley · Decided by Res 0614-2026 ↗ · View on Legistar ↗

Official title

Application number N 250254 ZRQ (50-20 108th Street Rezoning) submitted by Federici Builders Corp., pursuant to Section 201 of the New York City Charter, for an amendment of the Zoning Resolution of the City of New York, amending APPENDIX F for the purpose of establishing a Mandatory Inclusionary Housing Area, Borough of Queens, Community District 4, Council District 21.

Summary

This application would amend the zoning text to establish a Mandatory Inclusionary Housing area at 50-20 108th Street in Corona, Queens, alongside a related zoning map change. Together the actions would allow two new mixed-use buildings totaling about 216,400 square feet with 196 apartments, including 51 income-restricted units. The Council held a public hearing on June 17, 2026 and the Land Use Committee recommended approval on July 14, 2026.
