Int 1112-2026 (In committee: heard, awaiting a vote)

Law to raise spending caps for eight business districts

· Sponsor: Linda Lee
+ 1 co-sponsor Chris Banks
· By request of the Mayor · View on Legistar ↗

Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to authorizing an increase in the amount to be expended annually in eight business improvement districts

Summary

This bill would let eight Business Improvement Districts spend more each year starting July 1, 2027, including raising 34th Street's cap to $16.3 million and creating new caps for Southern Boulevard, Greater JFK and West Village. The increases would result in higher assessments on properties within each district. The eight districts are in Manhattan, Brooklyn, Queens and the Bronx.
