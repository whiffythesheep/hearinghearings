Official title

Resolution affirming New York City’s support for Canada, the contributions of Canadian New Yorkers, and the beneficial relationship between New York City and Canada

Summary

This resolution affirms New York City's support for Canada, Canadian New Yorkers and the economic and cultural relationship between the city and Canada amid US-Canada trade tensions. It cites Canada as a top trading partner, noting New York State imported $21.4 billion and exported $16.5 billion in goods to Canada in 2025, and that about 651,000 Canadians visit New York City annually while roughly 27,000 live there. The resolution calls for an end to hostile rhetoric and trade practices that it says harm New York City.
