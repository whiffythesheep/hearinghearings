Int 0256-2026 (In committee: heard, awaiting a vote)

Law to study a household textile recycling mandate

· Sponsor: Crystal Hudson
+ 4 co-sponsors Farah N. Louis, Gale A. Brewer, Shahana K. Hanif, Harvey D. Epstein
· View on Legistar ↗

Official title

A Local Law in relation to requiring the commissioner of sanitation to study the feasibility and potential environmental effects of a recycling mandate for household textiles

Summary

This bill would require the Sanitation Department to study and report, within one year of taking effect, on the feasibility and environmental effects of requiring households to separate textiles for recycling. The report would examine costs, market demand, environmental benefits and drawbacks, and whether a municipal textile recycling facility would be needed. In fiscal year 2025 the department collected nearly 187,000 tons of textile waste but diverted only about 4.3% of it.
