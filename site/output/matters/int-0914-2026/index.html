Law to study new PTSD therapy for veterans

Official title

A Local Law in relation to requiring a study and recommendations on the feasibility of implementing a pilot program to provide reconsolidation of traumatic memories therapy for veterans

Summary

This bill would require the Department of Veterans' Services, with the Health Department, to spend one year studying whether a pilot program offering reconsolidation of traumatic memories therapy could help city veterans with PTSD and other psychological disorders. The study would estimate how many veterans could benefit, analyze costs and funding sources, and determine if a pilot should move forward. Within six months of completing the study, the commissioner must submit recommendations to the Mayor and Council Speaker and post them online.
