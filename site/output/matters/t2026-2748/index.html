Official title

Oversight - Accessibility of City Parks, Playgrounds, and Recreation Centers.

Summary

The Council's Committees on Disabilities and on Parks and Recreation will hold a joint oversight hearing on accessibility of city parks, playgrounds and recreation centers. The hearing will examine the Parks Department's compliance with ADA accessibility standards, including a bill requiring annual accessibility reports, a bill on reduced admission fees for seniors, veterans and people with disabilities, and a bill expanding accessible playground swings. Background materials note the department oversees more than 30,000 acres of parkland, 2,000 parks, 1,000 playgrounds and 37 recreation centers.
