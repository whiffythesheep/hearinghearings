Resolution marking America's 250th anniversary

Passed Council · Sponsors: Julie Menin, David M. Carr, Farah N. Louis, Shanel Thomas-Henry, Amanda C. Farías, Carl Wilson, Frank Morano, Joann Ariola, Inna Vernikov · View on Legistar ↗

In brief

This resolution has the Council commemorate the 250th anniversary of the United States, marking the Declaration of Independence approved on July 4, 1776. It references national and state commemorations, including the federal America250 initiative and New York State's 250th Commemoration Act signed in 2022.
