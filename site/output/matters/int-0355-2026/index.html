Introduction

Int 0355-2026

Law to require composting of food waste by city agencies

Law or adopted · Sponsors: Sandy Nurse, Farah N. Louis, Althea V. Stevens, Rita C. Joseph, Crystal Hudson, Selvena N. Brooks-Powers, Shahana K. Hanif, Gale A. Brewer, Harvey D. Epstein, Chris Banks, Jennifer Gutiérrez, James F. Gennaro · View on Legistar ↗

In brief

This bill would designate organic waste as a recyclable material for New York City agencies and require the Department of Sanitation to set rules for source-separating organic waste generated when agencies prepare and serve meals. It would take effect 120 days after becoming law.

Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to organic waste recycling by city agencies
