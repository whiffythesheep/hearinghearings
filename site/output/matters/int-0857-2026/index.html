Law to require dog waste bag dispensers on litter baskets

Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to requiring the department of sanitation to install and refill dog waste bag dispensers on public litter baskets

Summary

This bill would require the Department of Sanitation to install at least 1,200 dog waste bag dispensers on public litter baskets across the five boroughs and keep them stocked, either through volunteer local groups or the department itself, until June 30, 2030. The department would need to report to the Council Speaker by March 1, 2030 on whether the program reduced complaints about dog waste and whether it should continue. The law would take effect 180 days after being enacted.
