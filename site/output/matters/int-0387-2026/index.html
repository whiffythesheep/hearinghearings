Int 0387-2026 (In committee: heard, awaiting a vote)

Law to require yearly crime reports from homeless shelters

· Sponsor: Public Advocate Jumaane Williams
+ 3 co-sponsors Kamillah Hanks, Farah N. Louis, Crystal Hudson
· View on Legistar ↗

Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to requiring reporting on crime statistics in shelters

Summary

This bill would require the Department of Homeless Services to publish an annual report on "critical incidents" in shelters, including assault, sexual assault, domestic violence, child abuse, weapons possession, arson and theft. The report would also cover security upgrades and any agreements with the police department or other agencies to improve shelter security, broken down by shelter type and population. As of October 2026, the City's shelter system housed over 85,000 people nightly.
