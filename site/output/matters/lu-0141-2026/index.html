Official title

Application number D 2550162086 SWQ (Cali Tajadas Bakery & Restaurant) pursuant to Section 19-160.2 of the Administrative Code of the City of New York, for a revocable consent to establish, maintain, and operate a sidewalk café located at 45-18 104th Street, Corona, NY 11368, Borough of Queens, Community District 4, Council District 21.

Summary

This application seeks a revocable consent for Cali Tajadas Bakery & Restaurant to establish, maintain and operate a sidewalk café at 45-18 104th Street in Corona, Queens. The site is in Community District 4 and Council District 21.
