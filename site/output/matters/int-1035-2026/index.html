Int 1035-2026 (In committee: awaiting a hearing)

Law to excuse residents cleaning up from sanitation tickets

· Sponsor: Simcha Felder
+ 2 co-sponsors Frank Morano, Joann Ariola
· View on Legistar ↗

Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to establishing an exemption from enforcement of certain residential sanitation violations

Summary

This bill would bar the Department of Sanitation from issuing violations, tickets or summonses for residential sidewalks and similar areas during scheduled enforcement periods if a resident is present and actively cleaning or getting ready to clean. It would apply to the existing 8-9 a.m. and 6-7 p.m. enforcement windows. The law would take effect 120 days after being enacted.
