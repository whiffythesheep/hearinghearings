Int 1080-2026 (In committee: heard, awaiting a vote)

Law to require disclosure of paid city social media posts

· Sponsor: Oswald J. Feliz
+ 3 co-sponsors David M. Carr, Gale A. Brewer, Phil Wong
· View on Legistar ↗

Official title

A Local Law to amend the New York city charter, in relation to requiring disclosures on third-party public communications paid for with government resources

Summary

This bill would require public servants to disclose when a third-party social media post, video or audio content was paid for with city funds, using language like "Paid Partnership with the City of New York" or the sponsoring agency's name. It applies to text, audio, video and captions, including translations into other languages. Intentional violations would be a misdemeanor, and the Campaign Finance Board and Conflicts of Interest Board could investigate and impose civil penalties up to $10,000 per violation.
