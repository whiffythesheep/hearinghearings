Int 0786-2026 (Passed Council: awaiting the Mayor)

Law to help jailed voters fix ballot errors

· Sponsor: Selvena N. Brooks-Powers
+ 14 co-sponsors Harvey D. Epstein, Nantasha M. Williams, Julie Won, Gale A. Brewer, Yusef Salaam, Lynn C. Schulman, Rita C. Joseph, Farah N. Louis, Lincoln Restler, Virginia Maloney, Mercedes Narcisse, Amanda C. Farías, Crystal Hudson, Public Advocate Jumaane Williams
· View on Legistar ↗

Official title

A Local Law to amend the New York city charter, in relation to procedures for early mail and absentee ballots for voters in department of correction custody

Summary

This bill would require the Department of Correction, working with the Board of Elections, to deliver notices of curable ballot defects to incarcerated voters and collect their signed corrections within 7 business days. It also requires the department to help voters get a new ballot when a defect cannot be cured. Starting January 1, 2027 the department must publish an annual report on cure notices, returns and uncounted ballots, without personal information.
