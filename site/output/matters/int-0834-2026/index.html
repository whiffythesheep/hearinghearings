Introduction

Int 0834-2026

Law to track M/WBE subcontracting in human services deals

In committee · Sponsors: Lincoln Restler, Althea V. Stevens, Nantasha M. Williams, Selvena N. Brooks-Powers · View on Legistar ↗

In brief

This bill would require contractors on city human services contracts to disclose to their contracting agency any subcontract executed with a certified minority- or women-owned business, including the subcontractor's contact information and the value and type of work, within 30 days of signing. Agencies would need to forward these disclosures to the Mayor's Office of Contract Services annually by October 31. The law would take effect 180 days after passage.
