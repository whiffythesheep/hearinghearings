Int 0834-2026 (In committee: heard, awaiting a vote)

Law to require reporting on minority- and women-owned subcontractors

· Sponsor: Lincoln Restler
+ 4 co-sponsors Althea V. Stevens, Nantasha M. Williams, Selvena N. Brooks-Powers, Harvey D. Epstein
· View on Legistar ↗

Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to reporting on minority- and women-owned business participation in subcontracts for human services contracts

Summary

This bill would require contractors on city human services contracts to disclose to the contracting agency within 30 days when they hire a certified minority- or women-owned business as a subcontractor, including the subcontractor's contact information and the value and type of work. Agencies would have to forward these disclosures to the Mayor's Office of Contract Services by October 31 each year for subcontracts executed or amended during the prior fiscal year. The law would take effect 180 days after enactment.
