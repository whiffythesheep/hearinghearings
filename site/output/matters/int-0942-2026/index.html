Law to give small businesses tech training

· Sponsor: Virginia Maloney
+ 6 co-sponsors Kevin C. Riley, Farah N. Louis, Lincoln Restler, Lynn C. Schulman, Shirley Aldebol, Frank Morano
· View on Legistar ↗

Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to establishing a program to provide small businesses with technology literacy training

Summary

This bill would require the Department of Small Business Services to create a program training small business owners on emerging technology, including artificial intelligence. The training would cover using such tools in operations, understanding related policies, and deciding whether to adopt them. The program must be established within one year of the law taking effect, which is 180 days after enactment.
