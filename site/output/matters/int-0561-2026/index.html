Proposed law

Int 0561-2026 (In committee: heard, awaiting a vote)

Law to reform how citizen idling complaints are handled

· Sponsor: James F. Gennaro · View on Legistar ↗

Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to regulating the idling of engines and the use of citizen's complaints to enforce laws enforced by the department of environmental protection

Summary

This bill would change the city's citizen air complaint program used to enforce engine idling rules, including new cure options letting violators avoid penalties by certifying installation of anti-idling technology, revised timelines for complaint review and adjudication, lower reward percentages for complainants (reduced from 25% to 12.5% and from 50% to 25%), and a new code of conduct for people filing complaints. It also updates definitions like bus, truck and school bus depot and extends the complaint response window from 45 to 90 days.
