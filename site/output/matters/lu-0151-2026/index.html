LU 0151-2026 (Reported from Committee)

Zoning text change to require affordable units at 100-12 Queens Boulevard

· Sponsor: Kevin C. Riley · View on Legistar ↗

Official title

Application number N 260185 ZRQ (100-12 Queens Boulevard Rezoning) submitted by 100 Queens Blvd Co. LLC, pursuant to Section 201 of the New York City Charter, for an amendment of the Zoning Resolution of the City of New York, amending APPENDIX F for the purpose of establishing a Mandatory Inclusionary Housing Area, Borough of Queens, Community District 6, Council District 29.

Summary

This action would designate a Mandatory Inclusionary Housing area at 100-12 Queens Boulevard in Forest Hills, Queens, working alongside a related zoning map change. Together the actions would enable a 21-story building with about 389 apartments, including 97 income-restricted units, and 16,000 square feet of commercial space. The Council's Land Use Committee recommended approval with modifications on November 10, 2026.
