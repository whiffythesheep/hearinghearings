Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to codifying the official flag of the borough of Staten Island

Summary

This bill would formally adopt the current flag design as the official flag of the borough of Staten Island in city law. The flag features a circular seal with a woman holding a sword and shield, ships on water and the words "Staten Island" and "City of New York" along with the dates 1609 and 1898. It would take effect immediately and could be displayed at civic functions such as parades and receptions.
