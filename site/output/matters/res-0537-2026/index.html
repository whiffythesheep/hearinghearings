Official title

Resolution celebrating the historical documents that contributed to the birth of the United States of America and are on display at the New York Public Library as part of the nation's 250th anniversary events

Summary

This resolution celebrates the New York Public Library's exhibition "Declaring America: 1776 and Beyond," running from June 15, 2025 through January 10, 2027, which displays historical documents tied to America's founding. It highlights the library's handwritten copy of the Declaration of Independence, on public view by reservation from July 1 to July 7, 2026. The Council resolution itself takes no binding action but honors the exhibit as part of the nation's 250th anniversary.
