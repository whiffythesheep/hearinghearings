T2026-2590

Law to require sidewalk-detection brakes on shared e-bikes/scooters

In committee · Sponsors: Shaun Abreu, Virginia Maloney, Mercedes Narcisse, Frank Morano · View on Legistar ↗

In brief

This bill would require companies operating electric bike and scooter share systems to install sidewalk riding detection technology on their devices. It would also require a safety feature that automatically and gradually slows a device to a stop if it detects the device riding on a sidewalk. A first violation carries no penalty, but repeat violations within two years could bring a civil penalty of up to $1,000.

Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to the installation of sidewalk riding detection technology on electric bicycles and electric scooters that are part of share systems
