LU 0125-2026 (Passed Council: adopted)

Rezoning to allow housing at 132 Melrose Street, Brooklyn

· Sponsor: Kevin C. Riley · Decided by Res 0675-2026 ↗ · View on Legistar ↗

Official title

Application number C 260135 ZMK (132 Melrose Street Rezoning) submitted by Melrose Towers Corp., pursuant to Sections 197-c and 201 of the New York City Charter for an amendment of the Zoning Map, Section No. 13b, changing from an M1-1 District to an M1-2A/R6A District and establishing a Special Mixed Use District (MX-22), Borough of Brooklyn, Community District 4, Council District 34.

Summary

This application would change the zoning map from an M1-1 manufacturing district to an M1-2A/R6A district with a Special Mixed Use District designation at 132-136 Melrose Street in Bushwick, Brooklyn. It would help enable a new six-story mixed-use building with 18 apartments, including five permanently income-restricted units, plus 6,000 square feet of ground-floor commercial space. The Council held a public hearing on August 12, 2026, and a related zoning text change would create a Mandatory Inclusionary Housing area.
