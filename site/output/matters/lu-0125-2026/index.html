Land Use Application

LU 0125-2026

Rezoning to allow housing at 132 Melrose Street, Brooklyn

Passed committee · Sponsor: Kevin C. Riley · View on Legistar ↗

In brief

This land use application would change the zoning map for a site at 132 Melrose Street in Brooklyn from a manufacturing district to a mixed manufacturing-residential district with a special mixed use designation. The change would let the area shift from purely industrial use toward residential and commercial development. It affects a site in Brooklyn Community District 4, Council District 34.
