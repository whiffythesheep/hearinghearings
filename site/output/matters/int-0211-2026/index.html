Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to requiring the department of health and mental hygiene to provide information regarding fertility treatment, including insurance coverage of fertility treatment

Summary

This bill would require the Department of Health and Mental Hygiene to run a public education campaign, by December 1, 2027, explaining fertility treatment options and insurance and Medicaid coverage for them. The campaign would use ads on the internet, subways and LinkNYC kiosks, and materials would be distributed to hospitals, clinics and health care providers citywide. Starting December 1, 2028, the department would have to update and redistribute these materials to providers each year.
