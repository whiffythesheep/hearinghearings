LU 0072-2026 (Passed Council: adopted)

Rezoning map change for 801 Eleventh Avenue, Manhattan

· Sponsor: Kevin C. Riley · View on Legistar ↗

Official title

Application number C 260013 ZMM (Dewitt Clinton Park North (801 Eleventh Avenue)) submitted by 801 11th Ave., LLC, pursuant to Sections 197-c and 201 of the New York City Charter for an amendment of the Zoning Map, Section Nos. 8a, 8b, and 8c, changing from an M2-4 District to a C4-7 District and establishing a Special Hudson River Park District (HRP), Borough of Manhattan, Community District 4, Council Districts 3 and 6.

Summary

This application would change the zoning map at 801 Eleventh Avenue from an M2-4 manufacturing district to a C4-7 commercial district and establish a Special Hudson River Park District. It is one of three related actions that would allow a mixed-use residential and commercial development totaling about 386,350 square feet of floor area. The change affects property in Manhattan Community District 4.
