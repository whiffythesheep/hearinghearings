Official title

A Local Law in relation to establishing a low-traffic neighborhood pilot program

Summary

This bill would require the Department of Transportation to run a 2-year pilot program installing at least two types of traffic calming devices, like speed humps or curb extensions, in five neighborhoods across at least three boroughs to redirect traffic away from residential streets. The department must consult local community boards on device placement and ensure access for emergency, delivery, sanitation and residents' vehicles. It must also study crash, injury, noise and complaint data in these areas and report findings and recommendations to the mayor and Council within six months after the pilot ends.
