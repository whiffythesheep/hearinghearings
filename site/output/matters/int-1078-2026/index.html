Int 1078-2026 (In committee: awaiting a hearing)

Law to limit Correction Department contact with ICE

· Sponsor: Elsie Encarnación
+ 7 co-sponsors Shahana K. Hanif, Tiffany L. Cabán, Alexa Avilés, Shekar Krishnan, Lincoln Restler, Carmen N. De La Rosa, Sandy Nurse
· View on Legistar ↗

Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to limiting communication between the department of correction and federal immigration authorities

Summary

This bill would bar the Department of Correction from communicating with federal immigration authorities about people in its custody, except when honoring a civil immigration detainer or when the communication is unrelated to immigration enforcement. It would take effect 60 days after becoming law.
