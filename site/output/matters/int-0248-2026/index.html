Introduction

Int 0248-2026

Law to make it easier to sign up for Fair Fares

Awaiting the Mayor · Sponsors: Crystal Hudson, Lincoln Restler, Julie Won, Carmen N. De La Rosa, Farah N. Louis, Gale A. Brewer, Shahana K. Hanif, Harvey D. Epstein, Virginia Maloney, Shanel Thomas-Henry, Justin E. Sanchez, Tiffany L. Cabán, Alexa Avilés, Mercedes Narcisse, Phil Wong, Shaun Abreu, Elsie Encarnación, Amanda C. Farías · View on Legistar ↗

In brief

This bill would require the Social Services Commissioner to create a streamlined enrollment process for Fair Fares NYC, letting eligible New Yorkers consent to use their SNAP or cash assistance application information to enroll. It would also require the city to notify eligible people about this process, try to reach agreements with state agencies to identify more eligible residents, study automatic enrollment in all city benefit programs, and report annually to the Council on enrollment and usage.

Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to streamlining enrollment of eligible individuals in fair fares
