Int 1019-2026 (In committee: awaiting a hearing)

Law to study how older adults use digital platforms

· Sponsor: Susan Zhuang
+ 4 co-sponsors Phil Wong, Lynn C. Schulman, Ty Hankerson, Shirley Aldebol
· View on Legistar ↗

Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to advertising programs and services on digital platforms used by older adults

Summary

This bill would require the Department for the Aging to study which digital platforms older adults use most, broken down by factors like ethnicity, gender, location and income, within 120 days of enactment. The department would then submit a plan within 150 days to advertise its programs and services on those platforms, and issue a report within one year assessing the outreach's effectiveness.
