Int 1118-2026 (In committee: heard, awaiting a vote)

Law to set privacy and disclosure rules for AI chatbots

· Sponsor: Frank Morano
+ 3 co-sponsors Virginia Maloney, Farah N. Louis, Mercedes Narcisse
· View on Legistar ↗

Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to chatbot data privacy, security, and transparency

Summary

This bill would require companies offering chatbots in New York City to secure user data, let users access their own chat logs and disclose clearly that they are talking to a bot rather than a person. It would bar chatbots from using personal data for ads or profiling without consent, prohibit claiming chatbot advice equals that of a licensed professional and require monthly safety risk assessments. Violations could bring civil penalties up to $25,000 and the law would take effect 180 days after enactment.
