Res 0108-2026

Resolution urging Albany to fund IVF with a tax credit

In committee · Sponsors: Frank Morano, Kayla Santosuosso, Farah N. Louis, Chris Banks, Jennifer Gutiérrez, Chi A. Ossé, Amanda C. Farías, Yusef Salaam, Tiffany L. Cabán, Vickie Paladino, Inna Vernikov, David M. Carr · View on Legistar ↗

In brief

This resolution calls on the New York State Legislature to pass, and the Governor to sign, S.5262/A.3051, creating a refundable tax credit for up to three cycles of in vitro fertilization not covered by insurance. It cites IVF costs ranging from $15,000 to $30,000 per cycle and notes state insurance mandates do not cover small-group, individual or exchange plans. The measure aims to ease financial burdens on New Yorkers pursuing fertility treatment.
