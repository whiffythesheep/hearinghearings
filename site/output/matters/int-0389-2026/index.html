Int 0389-2026 (In committee: heard, awaiting a vote)

Law to require delivery apps to check moped registration

· Sponsor: Lincoln Restler
+ 8 co-sponsors Farah N. Louis, Gale A. Brewer, Susan Zhuang, Lynn C. Schulman, Carl Wilson, Frank Morano, Joann Ariola, Vickie Paladino
· View on Legistar ↗

Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to requiring third-party food delivery services to verify the registration of mopeds used by food delivery workers

Summary

This bill would require third-party food delivery services to verify that mopeds used by their delivery workers are properly registered before letting workers use them for deliveries. It would take effect 60 days after becoming law. The measure is one of several micromobility-related bills being heard alongside an oversight hearing on regulation and safety of delivery vehicles.
