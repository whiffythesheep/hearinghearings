Int 0553-2026

Law to fund security systems for small retail stores

Law or adopted · Sponsors: Oswald J. Feliz, Amanda C. Farías, Mercedes Narcisse, Kamillah Hanks, Shaun Abreu, Farah N. Louis, Virginia Maloney, Shanel Thomas-Henry, Sandra Ung, Lynn C. Schulman, Chris Banks, Carl Wilson, James F. Gennaro · View on Legistar ↗

In brief

This bill would require the Department of Small Business Services, or another agency designated by the mayor, to establish a program giving financial assistance to owners of small retail businesses to help cover the cost of buying and installing security system technology such as cameras, plexiglass barriers or panic buttons. The program must be created within 6 months of the law taking effect, subject to funding. It applies to small ground-floor retail businesses that are not franchises.
