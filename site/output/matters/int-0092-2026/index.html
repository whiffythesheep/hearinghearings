Int 0092-2026 (Law: Local Law 119 of 2026)

Law to allow parking stickers for street cleaning violations

· Enacted July 30, 2026 · Sponsor: Gale A. Brewer
+ 4 co-sponsors Farah N. Louis, Chris Banks, Shahana K. Hanif, Carl Wilson
· View on Legistar ↗

Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to the use of adhesive stickers on motor vehicles in the enforcement of alternate side of the street parking rules

Summary

This bill would repeal the 2012 ban on using adhesive stickers to enforce alternate side parking rules, letting the Department of Transportation and the Department of Sanitation again place a sticker on vehicles that violate street cleaning parking rules. The sticker could be up to 8.5 by 11 inches and placed on a vehicle's window. DSNY had previously said the stickers deterred repeat violators and improved street cleanliness ratings.
