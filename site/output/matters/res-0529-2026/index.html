Resolution to update groups receiving Council discretionary funds

Law or adopted · Sponsor: Linda Lee · View on Legistar ↗

In brief

This resolution approves new or changed designations of organizations receiving discretionary funding under the City's fiscal 2023 through 2026 expense budgets. It covers various Council initiatives including local, youth, food pantry and foreclosure prevention programs. Some listed organizations have not yet completed required prequalification review.
