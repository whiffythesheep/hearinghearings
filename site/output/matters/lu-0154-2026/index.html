Special permit for Studio 54 rehabilitation, Manhattan

· Sponsor: Kevin C. Riley · Decided by Res 0738-2026 ↗ · View on Legistar ↗

Official title

Application number C 260212 ZSM (Studio 54 Rehabilitation and Text Amendment) submitted by Roundabout Theatre Company, pursuant to Sections 197-c and 201 of the New York City Charter for the grant of a special permit pursuant to Section 81-745 (Floor area bonus for rehabilitation of existing listed theaters) to facilitate rehabilitation of Studio 54 Theater, located on 254 West 54th Street (Block 1025, Lots 10 and 58), Borough of Manhattan, Community District 5, Council District 3.

Summary

This land use application grants Roundabout Theatre Company a special permit to add about 187,783 square feet of bonus floor area in exchange for rehabilitating the historic Studio 54 theater at 254 West 54th Street in Manhattan. It is tied to a related zoning text amendment and is subject to an environmental review finding no significant impact. The Council approved the City Planning Commission's decision on November 12, 2026.
