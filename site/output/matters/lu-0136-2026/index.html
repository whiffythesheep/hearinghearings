Zoning text change for Sojourner Truth-Mapes project, Bronx

· Sponsor: Kevin C. Riley · Decided by Res 0672-2026 ↗ · View on Legistar ↗

Official title

Application number N 240207 ZRX (Sojourner Truth – Mapes Rezoning) submitted by 2136 Crotona Parkway Housing Development Fund Corporation, pursuant to Section 201 of the New York City Charter, for an amendment of the Zoning Resolution of the City of New York, amending APPENDIX F for the purpose of establishing a Mandatory Inclusionary Housing Area, Borough of the Bronx, Community District 6, Council District 15.

Summary

This application would amend the city's Zoning Resolution to establish a Mandatory Inclusionary Housing area for the Sojourner Truth-Mapes Rezoning in the Crotona neighborhood of the Bronx, Community District 6. It pairs with a related zoning map amendment to enable new residential and mixed-use buildings at 2136 Crotona Parkway and at East 182nd Street and Mapes Avenue. The Council's Land Use Committee recommended approval on October 6, 2026.
