Land Use Application

LU 0122-2026

Zoning text change for 862-868 Kent Avenue, Brooklyn

Law or adopted · Sponsor: Kevin C. Riley · Resolution: Res 0661-2026 · View on Legistar ↗

In brief

This application would amend the Zoning Resolution's Appendix F to establish a Mandatory Inclusionary Housing area at 862-868 Kent Avenue in Brooklyn. It applies to a site in Community District 3 and Council District 33 and would require affordable housing in future residential development there.

Official title

Application number N 240284 ZRK (862-868 Kent Avenue) submitted by Kent Development LLC and 124 Taaffe LLC, pursuant to Section 201 of the New York City Charter, for an amendment of the Zoning Resolution of the City of New York, amending APPENDIX F for the purpose of establishing a Mandatory Inclusionary Housing Area, Borough of Brooklyn, Community District 3, Council District 33.
