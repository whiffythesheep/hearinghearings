Land Use Application

LU 0100-2026

Rezoning map change for 2950 West 24th Street, Brooklyn

Law or adopted · Sponsor: Kevin C. Riley · View on Legistar ↗

In brief

This application would amend the zoning map to change an R6 and R6/C1-2 district to R6, R7-3, and R7-3/C2-4 districts at 2950 West 24th Street in Coney Island, Brooklyn. Combined with related actions, it would facilitate an 18-story mixed-use building with 408 income-restricted apartments, ground-floor community and commercial space, and open space.

Official title

Application number C 250248 ZMK (2950 West 24th Street Rezoning) submitted by Ocean Towers Partners LLC, pursuant to Sections 197-c and 201 of the New York City Charter for an amendment of the Zoning Map, Section No. 28d, eliminating from within an existing R6 District a C1-2 District, changing from an R6 District to an R7-3 District, and establishing within the proposed R7-3 District a C2-4 District, Borough of Brooklyn, Community District 13, Council District 47.
