Special permit for towers at 110 East 138th Street, Bronx

· Sponsor: Kevin C. Riley · View on Legistar ↗

Official title

Application number C 260190 ZSX (110 East 138th Street/63 Exterior Street) submitted by the New York City Department of Housing Preservation and Development (HPD), pursuant to Sections 197-c and 201 of the New York City Charter for the grant of a special permit pursuant to Section 62-837 of the Zoning Resolution to modify the requirements of Section 23-432 (Height and setback requirements) and 23-433 (Standard setback regulations), the regulations of Section 87-322 (Base heights and transition heights) and 87-323 (Tower provisions), and the requirements of Section 62-332 (Rear yards and waterfront yards), in connection with the proposed development of two new mixed buildings on property located at 63 Exterior Street and 110 East 138th Street (Block 2323, Lots 28, 29, and 43), in an R7-2/C2-4 District, within the Special Harlem River Waterfront District (Core Subdistrict), Borough of the Bronx, Community District 1, Council District 8.

Summary

The city's housing agency HPD seeks a special permit to adjust height, setback, tower and rear yard rules for two new mixed-use buildings at 63 Exterior Street and 110 East 138th Street in the Bronx. The site sits in an R7-2/C2-4 district within the Special Harlem River Waterfront District's Core Subdistrict, in Community District 1 and Council District 8.
