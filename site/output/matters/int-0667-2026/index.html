Law to let people do community service instead of littering fines

· Sponsor: Nantasha M. Williams
+ 12 co-sponsors Althea V. Stevens, Farah N. Louis, Gale A. Brewer, Crystal Hudson, Selvena N. Brooks-Powers, Shahana K. Hanif, Yusef Salaam, Kamillah Hanks, Tiffany L. Cabán, Susan Zhuang, Jennifer Gutiérrez, Oswald J. Feliz
· View on Legistar ↗

Official title

A Local Law to amend the New York city charter, in relation to offering community service in lieu of civil penalties for certain littering violations

Summary

This bill would require administrative law judges at the Office of Administrative Trials and Hearings to offer community service instead of a civil fine for certain littering violations tied to a building or property. It applies to owners, lessees, tenants, occupants or persons in charge of the premises. The law would take effect 120 days after being enacted.
