Rezoning map change for 100 Queens Boulevard, Queens

· Sponsor: Kevin C. Riley · View on Legistar ↗

Official title

Application number C 260184 ZMQ (100-12 Queens Boulevard Rezoning) submitted by 100 Queens Blvd Co. LLC, pursuant to Sections 197-c and 201 of the New York City Charter for an amendment of the Zoning Map, Section No. 14a: eliminating from within an existing R4B District a C1-2 District; eliminating from within an existing R7-1 District a C1-2 District; changing from an R7-1 District to an R9X District; establishing within an existing R7-1 District a C2-4 District; and establishing within the proposed R9X District a C2-4 District, Borough of Queens, Community District 6, Council District 29.

Summary

This application would amend the zoning map in Queens Community District 6 to change an R7-1 district to R9X and adjust related commercial overlay districts near 100 Queens Boulevard. It would also eliminate certain existing C1-2 commercial overlay districts within the R4B and R7-1 zones. The change affects an area in Council District 29.
