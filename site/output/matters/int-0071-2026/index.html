Int 0071-2026 (In committee: heard, awaiting a vote)

Law to make scaffolding permit holders repair damaged trees

· Sponsor: Pierina Ana Sanchez
+ 5 co-sponsors Shaun Abreu, Farah N. Louis, Gale A. Brewer, Crystal Hudson, Shahana K. Hanif
· View on Legistar ↗

Official title

A Local Law to amend the New York city building code, in relation to requiring permit holders responsible for sidewalk sheds or scaffolding to repair or replace certain damaged city-owned trees

Summary

This bill would require the permit holder responsible for a sidewalk shed or scaffolding to repair or replace, within six months, any city-owned tree outside the property line that is damaged from its use or placement. The requirement would apply within the public right-of-way and take effect immediately upon becoming law.
