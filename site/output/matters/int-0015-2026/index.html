Int 0015-2026 (Law: Local Law 117 of 2026)

Law to stop repeat background checks for child care workers

· Local Law 117 of 2026, enacted July 30, 2026 · Sponsor: Shaun Abreu
+ 9 co-sponsors Farah N. Louis, Crystal Hudson, Shahana K. Hanif, Eric Dinowitz, Gale A. Brewer, Lynn C. Schulman, Chris Banks, Kamillah Hanks, Carl Wilson
· View on Legistar ↗

Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to requirements and reporting for background checks for child care providers, employees, and volunteers

Summary

This bill would bar the Health Department from requiring another background check for a child care provider, employee or volunteer if one was already completed within the past 5 years. Exceptions apply if the person has been out of city child care work for more than 180 consecutive days or if a check is otherwise required by law. The change is meant to reduce delays caused by the department's background check backlog, which has previously taken up to a year to clear.
