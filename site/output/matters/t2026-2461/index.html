Official title

Oversight - Examining the Restructure of the Mayor’s Office to End Domestic and Gender-Based Violence under the Office of Community Safety.

Summary

The Council will examine the Mayor's decision to place the Mayor's Office to End Domestic and Gender-Based Violence under the new Office of Community Safety, following Executive Order 15. Advocates worry the restructuring adds bureaucracy and downgrades the office's leadership from commissioner to director, while the office's $89.1 million fiscal year 2027 budget is set to move into the new agency's budget in fiscal year 2028. The hearing will also examine related bills on health screenings and hotline reporting, plus two resolutions.
