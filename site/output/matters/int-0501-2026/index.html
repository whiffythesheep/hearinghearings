Int 0501-2026 (In committee: heard, awaiting a vote)

Law to limit community board district managers to four-year terms

· Sponsor: Nantasha M. Williams · View on Legistar ↗

Official title

A Local Law to amend the New York city charter, in relation to the terms of employment for district managers

Summary

This bill would cap community board district managers' terms at four years, though they could be reappointed for successive terms. It would let borough presidents remove a district manager who misses half or more of a board's meetings in a year or fails to submit required annual reports or district needs statements. The law would take effect immediately.
