Introduction

Int 0031-2026

Law to require more businesses to separate food waste

Law or adopted · Sponsors: Shaun Abreu, Sandy Nurse, Farah N. Louis, Gale A. Brewer, Selvena N. Brooks-Powers, Shahana K. Hanif, Justin E. Sanchez, Harvey D. Epstein, Crystal Hudson, James F. Gennaro, Chris Banks, Carl Wilson · View on Legistar ↗

In brief

This bill would broaden the categories of businesses that the Department of Sanitation can require to separate and properly dispose of organic waste, removing prior size and seating thresholds for stores, restaurants, arenas and other establishments. The Department of Sanitation would have to designate additional covered establishments by December 1, 2026, with compliance required by June 1, 2027 or when the business's commercial waste zone takes effect. Waste carters would also have to inform newly covered establishments about donating excess edible food.

Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to expanding the categories of establishments that may be subject to requirements regarding the disposal of commercial organic waste, and to repeal subdivision h of section 16-306.1 of such code
