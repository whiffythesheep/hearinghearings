Proposed law

Int 0246-2026

Law to return jail commissary money in full at release

Passed Council · Sponsors: Crystal Hudson, Sandy Nurse, Public Advocate Jumaane Williams, Farah N. Louis, Gale A. Brewer, Shahana K. Hanif, Oswald J. Feliz, Lynn C. Schulman, Kayla Santosuosso, Selvena N. Brooks-Powers, Nantasha M. Williams, Alexa Avilés, Mercedes Narcisse, Harvey D. Epstein, Carl Wilson, Pierina Ana Sanchez, Virginia Maloney, Althea V. Stevens, Jennifer Gutiérrez, Amanda C. Farías, Julie Won · View on Legistar ↗

In brief

This bill would require the Department of Correction to tell people leaving custody how much money remains in their commissary account and return all unused funds in cash before release, rather than capping cash payouts at $200 with the rest by check. It also requires the department to explain what information is needed to transfer a person's funds if they are moved to another correctional facility. The law would take effect 180 days after being enacted.

Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to returning funds remaining in commissary accounts when incarcerated individuals are released from custody
