Summary

The application, submitted by the Department of Citywide Administrative Services and the Department of Parks and Recreation, seeks City Planning Commission approval to acquire and site-select dozens of privately owned properties across Queens Community District 2 for future park development. The goal is to close the "walk to a park" gap in the district. The City Planning Commission filed its decision on July 13, 2026, and the Council approved it on August 13, 2026.
