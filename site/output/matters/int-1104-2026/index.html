Int 1104-2026 (In committee: heard, awaiting a vote)

Law to require probation to explain court process to accused youth

· Sponsor: Selvena N. Brooks-Powers
+ 7 co-sponsors Farah N. Louis, Mercedes Narcisse, Oswald J. Feliz, Gale A. Brewer, Yusef Salaam, Shanel Thomas-Henry, Shahana K. Hanif
· View on Legistar ↗

Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to providing youth accused of crimes with information about the criminal legal process

Summary

This bill would require the city's Department of Probation to give young people accused of crimes, and their parents or guardians, a notice explaining the criminal legal process, including how cases might be adjusted, referred for prosecution or sentenced, and listing organizations that provide legal help. It would also require probation officers to read youth their rights before an intake interview, including that their statements cannot be shared with prosecutors or used as evidence before conviction. The law would take effect 90 days after passage.
