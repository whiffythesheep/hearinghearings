Introduction

Int 0880-2026

Law to require dog waste warning signs in parks and sidewalks

Law or adopted · Sponsors: Mercedes Narcisse, Shaun Abreu, Farah N. Louis, Shahana K. Hanif, Shirley Aldebol, Lynn C. Schulman, Amanda C. Farías, Tiffany L. Cabán, Crystal Hudson, Carl Wilson · View on Legistar ↗

In brief

This bill would require the Parks Department to post signs at park entrances, exits and facilities like playgrounds and dog runs stating the rule against leaving dog waste on the ground and its penalties. It would also require the Department of Transportation to post similar signs on sidewalks and pedestrian plazas where it receives 311 complaints about dog waste, in consultation with the Sanitation Department. Both agencies would report annually to the Mayor and Council Speaker on sign postings, and the law would take effect 90 days after enactment.

Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to the posting of signs regarding dog waste removal
