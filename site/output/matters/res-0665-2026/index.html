Official title

Resolution calling on the United States Senate to approve, and the President to ratify the Convention on the Rights of Persons with Disabilities and its Optional Protocol, in recognition of the Convention’s 20th anniversary

Summary

This resolution calls on the US Senate to approve and the President to ratify the UN Convention on the Rights of Persons with Disabilities and its Optional Protocol, marking the Convention's 20th anniversary in 2026. The US remains the only G7 country that has not ratified the treaty despite signing it in 2009. The Council notes nearly one million New Yorkers live with disabilities and that ratification would let local advocacy groups engage with the UN's monitoring committee.
