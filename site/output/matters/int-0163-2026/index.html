Law to study ferry service for Inwood

· Sponsor: Carmen N. De La Rosa
+ 7 co-sponsors Althea V. Stevens, Farah N. Louis, Shahana K. Hanif, Phil Wong, Christopher Marte, Harvey D. Epstein, Mercedes Narcisse
· View on Legistar ↗

Official title

A Local Law in relation to a study and report on the feasibility of providing ferry service to Inwood

Summary

This bill would require the Department of Transportation, working with the Department of Small Business Services and the city's Economic Development Corporation, to study the feasibility of ferry service to Inwood, Manhattan, including possible sites at the Hudson River beach marina and Sherman Creek. The study would assess local benefits, logistical challenges, possible solutions and projected costs. The DOT commissioner must report findings to the Mayor and Council Speaker within one year of the law taking effect.
