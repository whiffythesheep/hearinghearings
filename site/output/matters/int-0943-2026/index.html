Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to prohibiting the operation of horse drawn cabs and establishing a horse drawn cab industry transition plan, and to repeal sections 17-334.1, 19-174, 20-377, 20-377.1, 20-380, 20-381, 20-381.1, 20-381.2, 20-382 and subdivisions b, c, and d of section 20-383 of the administrative code of the city of New York, relating to carriage horses and horse drawn cabs

Summary

This bill would ban new or renewed licenses for carriage horses, horse-drawn cabs and their drivers starting June 1, 2028, ending any existing licenses on that date. It requires carriage horses be disposed of humanely, barring sale for slaughter or reuse in the industry. The city must create a transition plan by April 1, 2027 to help owners, drivers and stable workers, including possible compensation and workforce assistance.
