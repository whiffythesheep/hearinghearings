Official title

Resolution calling on the New York State Legislature to pass, and the Governor to sign, A03578/S00403 to establish a SNAP and cash assistance fraud victims compensation fund

Summary

This resolution calls on the New York State Legislature to pass, and the Governor to sign, A03578/S00403, which would create a permanent state fund to compensate victims of SNAP and cash assistance benefit fraud. It cites that New York City's Human Resources Administration approved 120,000 skimming claims worth $47.7 million from 2023 to 2024 under a temporary federal program that expired December 20, 2024. It notes that New Yorkers skimmed after that date currently have no way to recover stolen benefits.
