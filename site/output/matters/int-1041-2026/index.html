Int 1041-2026 (In committee: heard, awaiting a vote)

Law to require notice before removing public trash cans

· Sponsor: Ty Hankerson
+ 10 co-sponsors Mercedes Narcisse, Lincoln Restler, Nantasha M. Williams, Phil Wong, Harvey D. Epstein, Amanda C. Farías, Selvena N. Brooks-Powers, Lynn C. Schulman, Kevin C. Riley, Vickie Paladino
· View on Legistar ↗

Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to requirements for the removal of public litter baskets by the department of sanitation

Summary

This bill would stop the Department of Sanitation from removing a public litter basket solely because it was used for illegal dumping. It would require DSNY to notify the affected Council member and community board before removing a basket, giving them 10 business days to comment, and to consider those comments before acting. DSNY would also have to file a quarterly public report on the number and locations of baskets, removals and related littering complaints.
