Land Use Application

LU 0096-2026

Zoning map change for 50-20 108th Street, Queens

Law or adopted · Sponsor: Kevin C. Riley · Decided by Res 0613-2026 ↗ · View on Legistar ↗

In brief

This application would change zoning at 50-20 108th Street in Corona, Queens from R6B to R7A and establish a C2-4 commercial overlay. Combined with a related text amendment, it would facilitate two mixed-use buildings totaling about 216,400 square feet with 196 apartments, 51 of them income-restricted. The City Council held a public hearing on June 17, 2026.

Official title

Application number C 250253 ZMQ (50-20 108th Street Rezoning) submitted by Federici Builders Corp., pursuant to Sections 197-c and 201 of the New York City Charter for an amendment of the Zoning Map, Section No. 10b: eliminating from within an R6B District a C2-3 District; changing from an R6B District to an R7A District; and establishing within the proposed R7A District a C2-4 District, Borough of Queens, Community District 4, Council District 21.
