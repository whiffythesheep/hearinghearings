LU 0129-2026 (Passed Council: adopted)

Application to approve co-op rehab project at 48-50 Convent Avenue

· Sponsor: Kevin C. Riley · Decided by Res 0709-2026 ↗ · View on Legistar ↗

Official title

Application number G 260006 NUM (48-50 Convent Avenue (ANCP) - UDAAP) submitted by the New York City Department of Housing Preservation and Development (HPD) pursuant to Article 16 of the General Municipal Law for approval of an urban development action area project for properties located at 48 Convent Avenue (Block 1969; Lot 65); 50 Convent Avenue (Block 1969; Lot 66); 453-455 West 125th Street (Block 1966; Lot 44); 12-14 Old Broadway (Block 1982; Lot 65), Borough of Manhattan, Community District 9, Council District 7.

Summary

The Council approved HPD's urban development action area project to rehabilitate four buildings at 48-50 Convent Avenue, 453-455 West 125th Street and 12-14 Old Broadway in Manhattan's Manhattanville neighborhood. The project will convert the buildings into about 75 affordable cooperative units, with occupied apartments sold to existing tenants for $2,500 and vacant units priced for households earning up to 120% of area median income. Rehabilitation is expected to take about 42 months from closing to cooperative conversion.
