Official title

Resolution standing with the people of Puerto Rico to stop the construction of Project Esencia, a luxury development in Cabo Rojo

Summary

This resolution declares the City Council's opposition to Project Esencia, a planned $2 billion luxury hotel and residential development spanning over 2,000 acres and three miles of beaches in Cabo Rojo, Puerto Rico. It cites concerns over environmental harm to endangered species and wetlands, destruction of archaeological sites, strain on water supplies and gentrification. The resolution states the Council stands with Puerto Rican residents and diaspora groups, including in New York City, who have protested the project.
