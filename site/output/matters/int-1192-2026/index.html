Int 1192-2026 (In committee: awaiting a hearing)

Law to repay nonprofits for loan costs from city contract delays

· Sponsor: Linda Lee
+ 2 co-sponsors Susan Zhuang, Farah N. Louis
· View on Legistar ↗

Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to reimbursement of financing costs incurred by nonprofit contractors due to city-controlled delays

Summary

This bill would require city agencies to reimburse nonprofit contractors for interest and lender fees on loans taken out to keep operating while a city contract awaits official registration. Nonprofits could file claims within 180 days after the delay ends, and the City's chief procurement officer would report annually on claims received, approved and denied starting October 1, 2027. The law would take effect 180 days after passage and apply to contracts entered into or renewed after that date.
