Summary

This bill would co-name 26 thoroughfares and public places across all five boroughs to honor September 11th victims, first responders and memorials, based on requests from local Council Members. Sites include Firemen's Memorial in Manhattan, Angels Circle in Staten Island, Tribute Park in Queens and several FDNY units that suffered losses on 9/11. The names would be posted on signs near existing street signs without changing the official City Map designation.
