Official title

Resolution calling on the New York State Legislature to pass, and the Governor to sign, legislation to entitle New York City to participate in New York State Raise the Age program funding

Summary

This resolution calls on the New York State Legislature to pass, and the Governor to sign, legislation allowing New York City to participate in the state's Raise the Age reimbursement program. The city currently cannot access over $1.5 billion in state funds set aside to help localities cover costs of implementing the 2017 Raise the Age law, which ended automatic adult prosecution of 16- and 17-year-olds. City agencies estimate they have spent hundreds of millions of dollars implementing the law without state reimbursement since 2017.
