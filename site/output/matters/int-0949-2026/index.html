Law requiring city LGBTQI+ health resources and a provider map

· Sponsor: Lynn C. Schulman
+ 10 co-sponsors Farah N. Louis, Shirley Aldebol, Virginia Maloney, Harvey D. Epstein, Amanda C. Farías, Shekar Krishnan, Carl Wilson, Mercedes Narcisse, Sandra Ung, Kayla Santosuosso

Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to requiring the department of health and mental hygiene to create and make available on their website LGBTQI+ health and gender-affirming care resources and a searchable map of LGBTQI+ health services

Summary

This bill would require the Department of Health and Mental Hygiene to post online resources about LGBTQI+ health and gender-affirming care, including information on accessing LGBTQI+-affirming providers, obtaining and providing gender-affirming care and related rights. It would also require the department to maintain a public, searchable online map of LGBTQI+ health services showing each provider's address, hours, contact information, services offered and accepted insurance. The bill would take effect immediately upon passage.
