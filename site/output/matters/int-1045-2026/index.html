Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to requiring alternative invoicing methods during invoicing disruptions

Summary

This bill would require city agencies to accept invoices through a backup method if an electronic invoicing system breaks down for three or more consecutive business days. Agencies would have to notify contractors within ten business days of any disputed charges, and pay the undisputed portion by the required due date. The city's chief procurement officer would report annually, starting October 1, 2028, on such disruptions and the payments made because of them.
