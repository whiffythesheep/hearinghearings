Official title

Resolution calling upon the New York State Legislature to pass, and the Governor to sign, legislation extending the commencement and completion deadlines for benefits under the Affordable Housing from Commercial Conversions (AHCC) tax incentive program

Summary

This resolution calls on the New York State Legislature and Governor to extend the commencement and completion deadlines for the Affordable Housing from Commercial Conversions tax incentive program. The program currently requires conversions to begin by June 30, 2026 for the longest 35-year tax exemption and to finish by December 31, 2039. The Council argues more time would help convert more office space, estimated at 12.2 million square feet in Manhattan, into housing including affordable units.
