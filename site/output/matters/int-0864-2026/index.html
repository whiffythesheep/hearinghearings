Int 0864-2026 (Law: Local Law 140 of 2026)

Law to launch dog waste composting pilot at dog runs

· Enacted September 12, 2026 · Sponsor: Harvey D. Epstein
+ 5 co-sponsors Shahana K. Hanif, Shirley Aldebol, Chris Banks, Tiffany L. Cabán, Carl Wilson
· View on Legistar ↗

Official title

A Local Law in relation to establishing a pilot program for collecting dog feces at dog runs for the purpose of composting

Summary

This bill would require the Parks Department to run a pilot program collecting dog feces from dog runs it manages for composting, using volunteer-led groups to install collection bins and compostable bags. The pilot would run for at least two years, starting within 180 days of the law taking effect. Parks would later report to the Mayor and Council on how much waste was composted, where the compost was used and whether to expand the program.
