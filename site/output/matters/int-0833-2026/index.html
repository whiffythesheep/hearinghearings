Law

Int 0833-2026 (Law: Local Law 159 of 2026)

Law to end setback rule on Eastern Parkway Extension

· Enacted October 24, 2026 · Sponsor: Sandy Nurse · View on Legistar ↗

Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to restrictions on the location of buildings and other structures on Eastern parkway

Summary

This bill removes the 30-foot building setback requirement along Eastern Parkway Extension in Brooklyn, from Ralph Avenue to Bushwick Avenue, except on the southern side between Ralph Avenue and Pitkin Avenue where it remains. More than 155 tax lots along the extension already have buildings that do not comply with the current setback. The law would take effect immediately and apply to existing buildings and structures.
