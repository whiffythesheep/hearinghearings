Int 0847-2026 (Law: Local Law 109 of 2026)

Law to warn tourists about common NYC scams

· Enacted June 13, 2026 · Sponsor: Sandra Ung
+ 7 co-sponsors Lincoln Restler, Nantasha M. Williams, Shanel Thomas-Henry, Virginia Maloney, Chris Banks, Mercedes Narcisse, Farah N. Louis
· View on Legistar ↗

Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to providing outreach and education on consumer protection issues that affect tourists

Summary

This bill would require the Department of Consumer and Worker Protection to create an outreach and education program warning tourists about common scams, including safety tips, reporting contacts and complaint instructions, posted online in multiple languages and updated yearly. It also requires DCWP to develop special materials for tourists ahead of the 2026 FIFA World Cup by June 1, 2026. The bill also makes minor wording updates to related consumer education sections of the Administrative Code.
