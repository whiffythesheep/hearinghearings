Introduction

Int 0970-2026

Law to boost weekend cleanup in nightlife areas

In committee · Sponsors: Jennifer Gutiérrez, Virginia Maloney, Lincoln Restler, Christopher Marte, Harvey D. Epstein, Farah N. Louis · View on Legistar ↗

In brief

This bill would require the Department of Sanitation to designate certain busy nightlife areas, based on foot traffic, bar and club density and 311 complaints, as nightlife districts. At a community board's request, DSNY would add one extra day of street sweeping and litter basket service each weekend in that district. DSNY could revert to the normal schedule if cleanliness-related 311 complaints stay lower for 30 straight days.

Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to enhanced street cleaning and litter basket services in nightlife districts
