Official title

Oversight - Municipal Recruitment and Civil Service Pathways.

Summary

The Council's Civil Service and Labor Committee held an oversight hearing on how the City recruits people into civil service jobs, examining DCAS programs like the Civil Service Pathways Fellowship, the Civil Service Ambassador Program and the Public Service Corps. It reviewed outreach events, exam fee waivers, and efforts to reduce barriers such as unnecessary qualification requirements and residency rules for hard-to-recruit titles. The hearing invited testimony from DCAS, labor unions and the public on trends and remaining barriers to municipal hiring.
