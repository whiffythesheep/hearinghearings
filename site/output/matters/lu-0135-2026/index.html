Land use

LU 0135-2026

Zoning map change for Sojourner Truth-Mapes, Bronx

In committee · Sponsor: Kevin C. Riley · View on Legistar ↗

In brief

This land use application would amend the zoning map in Bronx Community District 6, changing an R7-1 district to R8, removing a C1-4 district and establishing a new C2-4 district. The change is tied to the Sojourner Truth-Mapes rezoning proposed by 2136 Crotona Parkway Housing Development Fund Corporation. It falls within City Council District 15.

Official title

Application number C 240206 ZMX (Sojourner Truth – Mapes Rezoning) submitted by 2136 Crotona Parkway Housing Development Fund Corporation, pursuant to Sections 197-c and 201 of the New York City Charter for an amendment of the Zoning Map, Section No. 3d: eliminating from within an R7-1 District a C1-4 District; changing from an R7-1 District to an R8 District; and establishing within a proposed R8 District a C2-4 District, Borough of the Bronx, Community District 6, Council District 15.
