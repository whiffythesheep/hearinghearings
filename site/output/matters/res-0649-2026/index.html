Resolution urging discounted AirTrain JFK fares for airport workers

· Sponsor: James F. Gennaro · View on Legistar ↗

Official title

Resolution calling upon the Port Authority of New York and New Jersey to offer an AirTrain JFK fare discount package to John F. Kennedy International Airport employees

Summary

This resolution calls on the Port Authority of New York and New Jersey to offer JFK airport employees a discounted AirTrain fare package. It notes that MTA's discontinuation of MetroCard sales as of December 2025 has left the roughly 40,000 JFK workers without an affordable alternative to the full $17.50 roundtrip AirTrain fare. The Council urges the Port Authority to establish a new discount option now that OMNY is replacing MetroCards.
