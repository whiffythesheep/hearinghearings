Introduction

Int 0230-2026

Law to fund home safety upgrades for seniors and disabled residents

In committee · Sponsors: Crystal Hudson, Farah N. Louis, Lynn C. Schulman · View on Legistar ↗

In brief

This bill would require the Department for the Aging, working with the Mayor's Office for People with Disabilities, to run a financial assistance program covering up to $10,000 per person for in-home safety modifications like grab bars, handrails and stair lifts. It would apply to low-income homeowners aged 60 or older or people with disabilities aged 18 or older, and the department would have to respond to applications within 60 days. The law would take effect one year after enactment, subject to City appropriation.

Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to a financial assistance program for in-home preventative adaptations
