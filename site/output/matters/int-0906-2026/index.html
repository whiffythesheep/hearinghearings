Law

Int 0906-2026 (Law: Local Law 154 of 2026)

Law to speed up response to dog waste complaints

· Enacted October 10, 2026 · Sponsor: Justin E. Sanchez
+ 4 co-sponsors Farah N. Louis, Amanda C. Farías, Tiffany L. Cabán, Frank Morano
· View on Legistar ↗

Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to dog waste complaints

Summary

This bill would require the Department of Sanitation, after receiving at least 3 complaints of dog waste on the same city block within 7 days, to send employees to either remove the waste or notify the responsible property owner and issue a violation. The measure aims to strengthen enforcement of existing sanitation rules against dog waste in public spaces. It would take effect immediately upon passage.
