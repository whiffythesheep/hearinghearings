LU 0123-2026 (Passed Council: adopted)

Rezoning to allow housing at 158-06 Northern Boulevard, Queens

· Sponsor: Kevin C. Riley · Decided by Res 0673-2026 ↗ · View on Legistar ↗

Official title

Application number C 260171 ZMQ (158-06 Northern Boulevard Rezoning) submitted by Northern 158 Holdings LLC, pursuant to Sections 197-c and 201 of the New York City Charter for an amendment of the Zoning Map, Section No. 10c: eliminating from within an existing R5B District a C1-2 District; changing from an R2 District to an R7A District; changing from an R5B District to an R7A District; and establishing within the proposed R7A District a C2-4 District, Borough of Queens, Community District 7, Council District 20.

Summary

This zoning map amendment would change R5B/C1-2 and R2 districts to R7A/C2-4 and R7A districts at 158-06 Northern Boulevard in Murray Hill, Queens. It would help enable a new 10-story mixed-use building with about 70,765 square feet of space and 76 apartments, including 19 income-restricted units. The Council held a public hearing on August 12, 2026 and the committee recommended approval with modifications on September 24, 2026.
