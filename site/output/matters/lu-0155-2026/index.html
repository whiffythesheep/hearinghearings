LU 0155-2026

Application for tax break at 300 West 17th Street, Manhattan

Passed Council · Sponsor: Linda Lee · Decided by Res 0704-2026 ↗ · View on Legistar ↗

In brief

This approves a property tax exemption for a building at Block 740, Lot 37 in Manhattan, owned by a housing development fund company under Article XI of the Private Housing Finance Law. The exemption would run for up to 40 years from July 1, 2026, with the owner instead paying a tax equal to 5 percent of gross rent. It would end early if the property stops being used for affordable housing under HPD's rules.

Official title
