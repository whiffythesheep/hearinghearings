Int 1145-2026 (In committee: awaiting a hearing)

Law to require annual audit of police domestic incident reports

· Sponsor: Linda Lee
+ 4 co-sponsors Amanda C. Farías, Gale A. Brewer, Lincoln Restler, Frank Morano
· View on Legistar ↗

Official title

A Local Law to amend the New York city charter, in relation to an annual audit on domestic incident reports

Summary

This bill would require the Department of Investigation to audit the NYPD's use of domestic incident reports annually and report findings to the Council. The report would include data on completion rates, missing fields, timeliness, referrals given to victims and orders of protection, broken down by borough, council district and precinct. The first audit would be due within 180 days of enactment.
