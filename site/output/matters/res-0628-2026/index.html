Resolution urging schools to teach about Pluto's reclassification

In committee · Sponsor: Frank Morano · View on Legistar ↗

In brief

This resolution calls on the city's Department of Education to mark the 20th anniversary of Pluto's 2006 reclassification as a dwarf planet, on August 24, 2026, by encouraging age-appropriate lessons on its discovery and classification history. It asks that programming address the ongoing scientific debate over Pluto's planetary status.

Official title

Resolution calling on the New York City Department of Education to commemorate the 20th anniversary of the reclassification of Pluto as a dwarf planet by encouraging age-appropriate instruction and educational programming on Pluto’s discovery, classification history, and the ongoing scientific debate over its planetary status
