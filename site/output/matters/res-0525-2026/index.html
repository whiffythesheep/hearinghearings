Official title

Resolution celebrating the "Knicks in five" as the New York Knicks win the 2026 NBA Championship

Summary

This resolution celebrates the New York Knicks defeating the San Antonio Spurs 94-90 in Game 5 on June 13, 2026 to win the NBA Championship, the team's first title since 1973 and third overall. It highlights standout performances by players including Finals MVP Jalen Brunson and OG Anunoby. It also notes the June 18 ticker-tape parade down the Canyon of Heroes, the first in Knicks history.
