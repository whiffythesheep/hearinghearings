Proposed law

Int 0489-2026

Law to create one online database for towed vehicles

Passed Council · Sponsors: Inna Vernikov, Farah N. Louis, Gale A. Brewer, Linda Lee, Yusef Salaam, Phil Wong, Mercedes Narcisse, Eric Dinowitz, Lynn C. Schulman, Frank Morano, Vickie Paladino, Joann Ariola, David M. Carr · View on Legistar ↗

In brief

This bill would require the Department of Finance and other designated city agencies to build a single online database where vehicle owners can look up any vehicle towed from public or private property by the city or a licensed tow operator. Owners could also opt in to text or email alerts when their vehicle appears in the database. Towing entities would have to post information within two hours of a tow, and the bill repeals the older rule requiring separate notice to local police precincts.

Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to requiring the creation of a centralized online database and notification system for towed vehicles, and to repeal section 20-528 of such code, relating to notification of a towed vehicle to a local police precinct
