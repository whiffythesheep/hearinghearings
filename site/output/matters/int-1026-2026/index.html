Int 1026-2026 (In committee: heard, awaiting a vote)

Law to set aside curb space for pedicab parking

· Sponsor: Gale A. Brewer
+ 2 co-sponsors Harvey D. Epstein, Shanel Thomas-Henry
· View on Legistar ↗

Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to creating designated curb space for pedicabs

Summary

This bill would require the Department of Transportation to let pedicabs use existing taxi relief stands and create at least 15 new curb stands reserved just for pedicabs in Midtown Manhattan. Pedicabs could park at these designated spots for up to one hour. The requirement would take effect within one year of the law's enactment.
