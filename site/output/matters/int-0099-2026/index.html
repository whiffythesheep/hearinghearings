Law to report on where homeless families are placed

· Sponsor: Gale A. Brewer
+ 15 co-sponsors Amanda C. Farías, Tiffany L. Cabán, Farah N. Louis, Sandy Nurse, Rita C. Joseph, Lynn C. Schulman, Phil Wong, Althea V. Stevens, Alexa Avilés, Christopher Marte, Shekar Krishnan, Jennifer Gutiérrez, Shanel Thomas-Henry, Vickie Paladino, Public Advocate Jumaane Williams
· View on Legistar ↗

Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to requiring the department of homeless services to report on the placement of families with children into shelters

Summary

This bill would require the Department of Homeless Services to publish an annual report on families with children placed in shelters, showing how many were placed in the same zip code or an adjoining zip code as their last address. The report must break down results citywide and by borough, and separately for families with children under age 3 versus older children. The first report would be due one year after the law takes effect.
