Official title

Resolution declaring March 6 as Carmen de Lavallade Day in the City of New York to celebrate her life and legacy and to honor her groundbreaking contributions to dance and theater as well as her role in advancing diversity and artistic excellence in the performing arts

Summary

This resolution would declare March 6 as Carmen de Lavallade Day in New York City, marking the birthday of the dancer, choreographer and actress who died on December 29, 2025 at age 94. It celebrates her contributions to dance and theater, including her work with Alvin Ailey and the Metropolitan Opera, and her role in advancing diversity in the performing arts.
