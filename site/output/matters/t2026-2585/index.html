Official title

Oversight - Waste Equity and Environmental Justice.

Summary

The Council's Sanitation Committee held an oversight hearing on waste equity and environmental justice, examining DSNY's uneven implementation of the 2018 Waste Equity Law that was meant to reduce overconcentration of waste transfer stations in four community districts. The hearing also covered five bills, including measures on city-owned building waste containers, disposal of e-bike batteries, public organic waste receptacles, resident association waste containers and removal of public litter baskets. Data shows the four targeted districts still handle about 74 percent of the city's waste transfer throughput as of 2025.
