Int 0831-2026 (In committee: heard, awaiting a vote)

Law to give leave for bone marrow and organ donation

· Sponsor: Frank Morano
+ 12 co-sponsors Virginia Maloney, Farah N. Louis, Christopher Marte, Selvena N. Brooks-Powers, Yusef Salaam, Mercedes Narcisse, Kamillah Hanks, Phil Wong, Nantasha M. Williams, Kevin C. Riley, Vickie Paladino, Joann Ariola
· View on Legistar ↗

Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to providing leave for bone marrow and living organ donation and establishing a city bone marrow and living organ donor honor roll

Summary

This bill would require New York City employers to provide employees up to 5 business days of unpaid leave for bone marrow donation and up to 20 business days of unpaid leave for living organ donation, in addition to existing sick leave. Subject to funding, the City would create a wage-replacement program to pay employees for this leave and would bar employers from requiring makeup work or replacement coverage. It also directs the Department of Health and Mental Hygiene to create a voluntary honor roll recognizing City employees who donate bone marrow or organs.
