Int 0418-2026

Law to add in-person help applying for affordable housing

In committee · Sponsors: Pierina Ana Sanchez, Farah N. Louis, Oswald J. Feliz, Crystal Hudson, Jennifer Gutiérrez, Amanda C. Farías, Mercedes Narcisse, Sandy Nurse, Shekar Krishnan, Tiffany L. Cabán, Lynn C. Schulman, Lincoln Restler, Shahana K. Hanif, Selvena N. Brooks-Powers, Sandra Ung · View on Legistar ↗

In brief

This bill would require the Department of Housing Preservation and Development, with other agencies, to create a free in-person assistance program to help people apply for affordable housing through the Housing Connect portal. The program would be offered weekly at public locations with computer access, such as older adult centers, recreation centers, libraries and community centers, in at least one location per community district. HPD would also have to conduct outreach in English and other designated citywide languages to promote the program.

Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to requiring the department of housing preservation and development to create an in-person housing portal assistance program
