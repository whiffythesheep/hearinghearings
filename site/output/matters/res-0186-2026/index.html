Official title

Resolution designating November 28 annually as Albanian Independence Day in the City of New York

Summary

This resolution would designate November 28 of each year as Albanian Independence Day in New York City. It recognizes Albania's declaration of independence on November 28, 1912 and the roughly 100,000 New Yorkers of Albanian heritage living across the five boroughs, especially in the Bronx and Staten Island.
