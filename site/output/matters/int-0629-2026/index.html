Int 0629-2026 (In committee: heard, awaiting a vote)

Law to screen detained youth for early release

· Sponsor: Lincoln Restler
+ 2 co-sponsors Althea V. Stevens, Farah N. Louis
· View on Legistar ↗

Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to the establishment of teams to review the population of youth held in secure detention facilities

Summary

This bill would require the Administration for Children's Services to set up borough-based teams that review the cases of youth held in secure detention for at least 7 days and recommend candidates for early release or case resolution. The teams, which would include ACS, Probation, Department of Education and other agency staff, must meet twice a month and share findings with each youth's defense attorney. ACS would also have to publish a public report twice a year on how many youth were reviewed, recommended for release and actually released.
