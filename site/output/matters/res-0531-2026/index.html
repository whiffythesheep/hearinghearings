Res 0531-2026 (Passed Council: adopted)

Resolution to set 9% late-tax interest rate for mid-value properties

· Sponsor: Linda Lee · View on Legistar ↗

Official title

Resolution to establish that the interest rate be nine percent per annum for Fiscal Year 2027 for non-payment of taxes on properties with an assessed value of more than $250,000 but less than or equal to $450,000, or more than $250,000 but less than or equal to $450,000 per residential unit for cooperative apartments

Summary

This resolution sets the interest rate at 9 percent per year for fiscal year 2027 on unpaid property taxes for properties assessed between $250,000 and $450,000, or co-ops valued similarly per unit. The rate matches the level in place since fiscal year 2025, despite the Banking Commission recommending 15 percent. The Council notes delinquency has been declining, suggesting the current rate is already effective.
