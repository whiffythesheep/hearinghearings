Int 0135-2026 (In committee: heard, awaiting a vote)

Law to speed up posting of child care closures and inspections

· Sponsor: Tiffany L. Cabán
+ 3 co-sponsors Jennifer Gutiérrez, Farah N. Louis, Lynn C. Schulman
· View on Legistar ↗

Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to inspections and routine closures of child care centers

Summary

This bill would require the Health Department to post closing orders at the entrance of any child care center it shuts down for imminent health hazards and to give copies to parents picking up children. It would also require the department to post summary inspection reports online within 24 hours of each inspection. The law would take effect 60 days after enactment.
