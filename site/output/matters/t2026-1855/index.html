Hearing on housing instability for LGBTQ+ New Yorkers

Closed · View on Legistar ↗

In brief

The Council's Women and Gender Equity and General Welfare committees will examine housing insecurity and homelessness among LGBTQ+ New Yorkers, including access to shelters run by the Department of Homeless Services and the Department of Youth and Community Development. Officials from these agencies, the Human Resources Administration and the Mayor's Office of LGBTQIA+ Affairs are invited to testify on shelter conditions, discrimination and training for staff. The hearing will also review implementation of the 2021 Lopez settlement, which required the city to create dedicated shelter beds and protections for transgender and gender non-conforming people.
