Int 0787-2026 (In committee: heard, awaiting a vote)

Law requiring NYPD to report on targeted traffic enforcement

· Sponsor: Selvena N. Brooks-Powers
+ 9 co-sponsors Harvey D. Epstein, Lynn C. Schulman, Farah N. Louis, Susan Zhuang, Carl Wilson, Shirley Aldebol, Frank Morano, Joann Ariola, Vickie Paladino
· View on Legistar ↗

Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to reporting on targeted traffic enforcement operations by the police department

Summary

This bill would require the NYPD to report quarterly, starting July 1, 2026, on any targeted traffic enforcement operations, including where they occurred, how decisions were made and the types of summonses issued. It would also require reporting on enforcement specifically directed at e-scooters and e-bikes, and comparisons to periods without targeted enforcement. Reports would be sent to the Council speaker and posted on the NYPD website.
