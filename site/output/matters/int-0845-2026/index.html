Int 0845-2026

Law to require lighting under elevated train lines

In committee · Sponsors: Shanel Thomas-Henry, Ty Hankerson, Justin E. Sanchez, Oswald J. Feliz, Lincoln Restler, Tiffany L. Cabán, Amanda C. Farías, Shirley Aldebol, Shekar Krishnan, Althea V. Stevens, Sandra Ung, Harvey D. Epstein, Alexa Avilés, Selvena N. Brooks-Powers, Julie Won, Jennifer Gutiérrez, Rita C. Joseph, Kayla Santosuosso, Yusef Salaam, Crystal Hudson, Lynn C. Schulman, Phil Wong, Farah N. Louis, Gale A. Brewer, Shahana K. Hanif, Sandy Nurse, Carmen N. De La Rosa, Kevin C. Riley, Frank Morano, Joann Ariola · View on Legistar ↗

In brief

This bill would require the Department of Transportation to assess streets and sidewalks under elevated train lines within one year to determine if they lack sufficient lighting and report the results. Where lighting is found insufficient, DOT would have to install distinctive lighting fixtures within three years, subject to funding and approvals. Starting April 1, 2030, DOT would issue annual reports on lighting installations for ten years.

Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to requiring the department of transportation to install lighting under elevated train lines
