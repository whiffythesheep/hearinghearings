Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to requiring the department of transportation to install lighting under elevated train lines

Summary

This bill would require the Department of Transportation to assess streets and sidewalks under elevated train lines run by the MTA or NYC Transit for adequate lighting, and publish the results within one year. Where lighting is found lacking, DOT would have to install distinctive lighting fixtures within three years, subject to funding and approvals. Starting April 1, 2030, DOT would issue annual reports on completed installations for 10 years.
