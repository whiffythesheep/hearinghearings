Official title

Oversight - NYPD Crime Statistics.

Summary

The Council's Committee on Public Safety held an oversight hearing on how the NYPD collects, analyzes and publishes crime statistics, including CompStat and Index Crime data. The hearing examined recent trends showing overall crime declines since the pandemic, including record-low shootings and murders in 2025-2026, alongside a persistent rise in felony assaults and continued high violence in parts of the Bronx. The Committee sought to review NYPD's data practices and identify areas needing further attention.
