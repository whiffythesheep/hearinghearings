Law to require schools to publish admissions rules online

Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to requiring the disclosure of school admissions policies and procedures

Summary

This bill would require the Department of Education to post plain-language admissions policies and procedures for each school on its website, updated at least once per year. It covers the application process, priority groups and selection criteria, and how applications are evaluated. The law would take effect 90 days after enactment.
