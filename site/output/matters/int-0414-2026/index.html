Official title

A Local Law to amend the New York city charter, in relation to the creation of a three-digit gun violence intervention hotline

Summary

This bill would require the city's gun violence prevention office to set up a 24/7, three-digit hotline connecting callers with mental health and support services. The office would also have to run a public awareness campaign aimed at young adults and file annual reports on the hotline's call volume and effectiveness starting April 1. It would take effect 120 days after becoming law.
