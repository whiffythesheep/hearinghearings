Law to require training for doctors on uterine fibroids

· Sponsor: Farah N. Louis

Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to developing and conducting trainings for health care providers about uterine fibroids

Summary

This bill would require the city Health Department to develop and conduct trainings for health care providers on diagnosing and treating uterine fibroids, including on implicit bias and informed consent. The department would conduct outreach to inform providers of the trainings and could help incorporate them into Health and Hospitals Corporation professional development. The department must report annually on the trainings and the number of providers who completed them, starting within one year of the law taking effect.
