Zoning text change for 132 Melrose Street, Brooklyn

· Sponsor: Kevin C. Riley · Decided by Res 0676-2026 ↗ · View on Legistar ↗

Official title

Application number N 260136 ZRK (132 Melrose Street Rezoning) submitted by Melrose Towers Corp., pursuant to Section 201 of the New York City Charter, for an amendment of the Zoning Resolution of the City of New York, amending APPENDIX F for the purpose of establishing a Mandatory Inclusionary Housing Area, Borough of Brooklyn, Community District 4, Council District 34.

Summary

This application establishes a Mandatory Inclusionary Housing Area at 132-136 Melrose Street in Bushwick, Brooklyn, pairing with a related zoning map change. Together the actions would enable a six-story mixed-use building with 18 apartments, five permanently income-restricted, and 6,000 square feet of ground-floor commercial space.
