Law to make it easier to seal unsafe e-bike shops

· Sponsor: Susan Zhuang
+ 4 co-sponsors Vickie Paladino, Shanel Thomas-Henry, Frank Morano, Farah N. Louis
· View on Legistar ↗

Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to department of consumer and worker protection and fire department authority to seal the premises of retailers of powered bicycles, powered mobility devices, or storage batteries

Summary

This bill would let the Department of Consumer and Worker Protection or the Fire Department seal a retailer's premises after just two safety violations within two years, instead of the current three violations within three years. It applies to sellers, lessors and renters of powered bicycles, powered mobility devices and their storage batteries. The change would take effect immediately upon passage.
