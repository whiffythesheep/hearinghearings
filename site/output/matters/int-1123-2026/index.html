Int 1123-2026 (In committee: heard, awaiting a vote)

Law to protect whistleblowers reporting AI safety risks

· Sponsor: Kevin C. Riley
+ 5 co-sponsors Virginia Maloney, Farah N. Louis, Julie Won, Mercedes Narcisse, Frank Morano
· View on Legistar ↗

Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to city contractors and subcontractors posting information concerning whistleblower protections and clarifying whistleblower protections for reporting conduct related to the use or development of an artificial intelligence model

Summary

This bill would extend New York City's whistleblower protections to city employees and city contractor employees who report AI-related conduct they believe poses a substantial risk to public health or safety. City contractors and subcontractors would have to post notices at work sites about these whistleblower protections. The Department of Investigation would have to include the number of AI-related whistleblower reports in its annual report to the mayor and Council speaker.
