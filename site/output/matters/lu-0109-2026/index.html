Zoning text change to require affordable housing at 1166 Bedford Avenue

· Sponsor: Kevin C. Riley · View on Legistar ↗

Official title

Application number N 260163 ZRK (1166 Bedford Avenue Rezoning) submitted by Khalifah Residences LLC, pursuant to Section 201 of the New York City Charter, for an amendment of the Zoning Resolution of the City of New York, amending APPENDIX F for the purpose of establishing a Mandatory Inclusionary Housing Area, Borough of Brooklyn, Community District 3, Council District 36.

Summary

This application would establish a Mandatory Inclusionary Housing area for a site at 1166 Bedford Avenue in Bedford-Stuyvesant, Brooklyn. Combined with a related zoning map change, it would support a new 12-story building with about 144 income-restricted apartments, 13,400 square feet of community facility space and 4,800 square feet of ground-floor retail. The City Planning Commission approved the application on June 17, 2026, and the Council's Land Use Committee recommended approval on July 14, 2026.
