Int 1130-2026 (In committee: heard, awaiting a vote)

Law to require honesty in AI advertising

· Sponsor: Carl Wilson
+ 4 co-sponsors Virginia Maloney, Farah N. Louis, Mercedes Narcisse, Tiffany L. Cabán
· View on Legistar ↗

Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to requiring certain disclosures and prohibiting deceptive representations in the promotion of artificial intelligence models

Summary

This bill would require ads promoting an artificial intelligence model in New York City to disclose whether the model was validated by a third party. It would also ban ads that make materially false or misleading claims about an AI model's safety risks or safeguards. Violators could face civil penalties of up to $25,000 per violation, enforced by the city's Corporation Counsel.
