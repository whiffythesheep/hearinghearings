Int 0951-2026 (In committee: heard, awaiting a vote)

Law to bar officials from insider trading on prediction markets

· Sponsor: Shanel Thomas-Henry
+ 6 co-sponsors Justin E. Sanchez, Tiffany L. Cabán, Lincoln Restler, Carl Wilson, Shirley Aldebol, Frank Morano
· View on Legistar ↗

Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to preventing policymakers and their relatives from trading on prediction markets using insider information

Summary

This bill would ban the mayor, comptroller, public advocate, Council members, borough presidents and other high-ranking city officials, along with their close relatives, from trading prediction market contracts using non-public information they gain through their jobs. Officials would have to annually disclose any prediction market transaction of $50 or more, including its amount, date, subject and any profit or loss. Violations could bring a civil penalty of up to $25,000, repayment of any gains, and possible loss of public office, and the law would take effect 180 days after enactment.
