Int 1064-2026 (In committee: heard, awaiting a vote)

Law to set deadlines for City approvals on small construction projects

· Sponsor: Carl Wilson
+ 6 co-sponsors Lincoln Restler, Ty Hankerson, Shahana K. Hanif, Justin E. Sanchez, Kamillah Hanks, Frank Morano
· View on Legistar ↗

Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to timeframes for agency approvals and change orders on small capital projects

Summary

This bill would require the Department of Design and Construction, working with the Mayor's Office of Contract Services, to set timeframes for City agencies to approve work and decide on change orders for capital projects costing up to $10 million. Agencies that miss a deadline would have to give contractors a written explanation within 10 days, including when a decision is expected. The timeframes would be published online and reviewed at least every two years, with the law taking effect one year after passage.
