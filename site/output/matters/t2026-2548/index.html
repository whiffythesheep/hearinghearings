Official title

Oversight - Commission on Human Rights Disability Enforcement Trends.

Summary

The Council's Committees on Disabilities and Civil and Human Rights held a joint oversight hearing on how the NYC Commission on Human Rights enforces disability discrimination protections under the city's human rights law. The hearing examined complaint trends, noting disability was the most common basis for discrimination claims in fiscal year 2025 with 662 inquiries and 131 substantiated claims, and reviewed coordination between the Commission and the Mayor's Office for People with Disabilities. The committees also heard related bills on gender definitions in the human rights law, service animal access guidance, and resolutions on disability rights.
