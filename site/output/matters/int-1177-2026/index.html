Int 1177-2026 (In committee: heard, awaiting a vote)

Law to expand discounted park fees to veterans and military families

· Sponsor: Lynn C. Schulman
+ 2 co-sponsors Mercedes Narcisse, Frank Morano
· View on Legistar ↗

Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to requiring the department of parks and recreation to charge senior citizens, young adults, active members of the military, veterans and their minor children and persons with disabilities reduced admission fees to recreational facilities

Summary

This bill would require the Parks Department to charge reduced admission and membership fees at all recreational facilities, including recreation centers, museums, zoos and botanical gardens, to seniors 62 and older, young adults ages 18 to 24, people with disabilities, active military members and veterans and their minor children. Reduced fees could be no more than 25 percent of the highest fee charged at that facility. It would take effect 180 days after becoming law.
