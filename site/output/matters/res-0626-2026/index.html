Official title

Resolution calling upon the United States Senate to pass, and the President to sign, S.4814, to require the Secretary of Homeland Security to designate Haiti for Temporary Protected Status

Summary

This resolution calls on the US Senate to pass, and the President to sign, S.4814, which would require the Secretary of Homeland Security to designate Haiti for Temporary Protected Status until three months after January 20, 2029. It follows a June 2026 Supreme Court ruling that allowed the federal government to end TPS for Haiti, affecting an estimated 350,000 Haitian nationals nationwide including about 40,000 in New York City. The resolution notes Haitian New Yorkers are the city's second-largest Haitian immigrant community, concentrated in Brooklyn's Little Haiti.
