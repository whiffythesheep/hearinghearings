Official title

Oversight - Implementation of the Pied-à-Terre Tax.

Summary

The Council's Governmental Operations and Finance committees held an oversight hearing on the implementation of the new pied-à-terre tax on high-value non-primary residences, which took effect under the state's fiscal year 2027 budget. The hearing examined the Department of Finance's process for identifying and notifying property owners, noting that DOF sent warning letters to about 17,000 owners though only around 24,000 of nearly one million flagged properties actually meet the tax's value thresholds. It also addressed complaints about a rushed appeals timeline, later extended to September 18, 2026, and lawsuits challenging the rollout.
