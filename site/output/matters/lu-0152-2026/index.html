LU 0152-2026 (Passed Council: adopted)

Zoning text change for open space in South Richmond, Staten Island

· Sponsor: Kevin C. Riley · Decided by Res 0736-2026 ↗ · View on Legistar ↗

Official title

Application number N 260362 ZRR (South Richmond Designated Open Space Amendment) submitted by NYC Department of City Planning, pursuant to Section 201 of the New York City Charter, for an amendment of the Zoning Resolution of the City of New York, amending the Open Space provisions of Article X, Chapter 7 (Special South Richmond Development District), Borough of Staten Island, Community District 3, Council District 51.

Summary

The City Planning Commission's decision would adjust Designated Open Space boundaries within the Special South Richmond Development District in the Prince's Bay and Arden Heights neighborhoods of Staten Island. The amendment also updates outdated and contradictory zoning text to align with the 2023 South Richmond Zoning Relief changes. A city review found the change would not create new environmental impacts beyond those already studied in 2023.
