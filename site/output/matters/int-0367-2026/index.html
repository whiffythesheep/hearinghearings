Int 0367-2026

Law to require city waste stations to accept commercial trash

In committee · Sponsors: Sandy Nurse, Alexa Avilés, Farah N. Louis, Althea V. Stevens, Rita C. Joseph, Justin E. Sanchez, Gale A. Brewer, Crystal Hudson, Selvena N. Brooks-Powers, Shahana K. Hanif, Sandra Ung, Amanda C. Farías, Julie Won, Chi A. Ossé, Yusef Salaam, Carl Wilson · View on Legistar ↗

In brief

This bill would require the Department of Sanitation to accept and process commercial solid waste at all city-owned or operated marine and rail transfer stations. It would also require DSNY to publicly report annually on the amount and type of waste received at each station, its processing capacity and efforts to attract commercial waste. The law would take effect immediately upon passage.
