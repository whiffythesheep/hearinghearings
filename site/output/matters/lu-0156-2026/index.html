LU 0156-2026 (Passed Council: adopted)

Application for tax break for MHANY housing rehab, Bronx

· Sponsor: Linda Lee · Decided by Res 0705-2026 ↗ · Decided by Res 0706-2026 ↗ · View on Legistar ↗

Official title

MHANY Cluster 2.NR Rehab: Block 2265 Lots 2 and 3; Block 2282, Lot 12; Block 3107, Lot 29; Block 2620, Lot 34, Bronx, Community Districts 1, 3, and 6, Council Districts 8, 15, and 17.

Summary

This application would approve a real property tax exemption for a cluster of buildings at four Bronx sites being rehabilitated by a housing development fund company under the MHANY Cluster 2 program. The exemption, authorized under Section 577 of the Private Housing Finance Law, would run for up to seven years from an effective date of February 4, 2020, and could end early if the property is not operated under its regulatory agreement. It applies to land and existing buildings but not to future construction.
