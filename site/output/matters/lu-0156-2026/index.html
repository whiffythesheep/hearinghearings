LU 0156-2026

Application for tax break on Bronx affordable housing rehab

Passed Council · Sponsor: Linda Lee · Decided by Res 0705-2026 ↗ · Decided by Res 0706-2026 ↗ · View on Legistar ↗

In brief

This application would grant a property tax exemption under Section 577 of the Private Housing Finance Law for buildings at four Bronx sites being rehabilitated as part of the MHANY Cluster 2.NR Rehab project. The exemption would run for up to seven years from an effective date of February 4, 2020, so long as the property remains owned by a qualifying housing development fund company and complies with its regulatory agreement with the Department of Housing Preservation and Development.

Official title

MHANY Cluster 2.NR Rehab: Block 2265 Lots 2 and 3; Block 2282, Lot 12; Block 3107, Lot 29; Block 2620, Lot 34, Bronx, Community Districts 1, 3, and 6, Council Districts 8, 15, and 17.
