Law to create a committee reviewing city labor bargaining practices

· Sponsor: Lincoln Restler
+ 1 co-sponsor Nantasha M. Williams
· View on Legistar ↗

Official title

A Local Law to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to establishing a citywide collective bargaining assessment committee for the purpose of evaluating the City’s collective bargaining practices

Summary

This bill would establish a citywide collective bargaining assessment committee made up of city officials, a labor expert and a union representative to evaluate how the City negotiates contracts for its lowest-paid employees. Each year by January 1 the committee would review wages and job conditions for the lowest-paid quarter of the municipal workforce, and by April 30 issue public recommendations for at least three job titles. The law would take effect 60 days after becoming law.
