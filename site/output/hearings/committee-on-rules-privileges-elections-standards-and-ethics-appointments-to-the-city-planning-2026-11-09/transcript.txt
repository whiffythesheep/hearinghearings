Good morning. The Board of Corrections is established under Section 626 of the City Charter and is responsible for the inspection of all institutions and facilities under the jurisdiction of the Department of Correction, and of all records relating to that work. In addition, the Board of Corrections is responsible for the evaluation of the Department of Correction's performance against minimum standards. The Board promulgates such minimum standards and rules and regulations for the care, custody, correction, treatment, supervision, and discipline of all persons held or confined under the jurisdiction of the Department. It promulgates such minimum standards and rules and regulations after giving the Mayor, the Commissioner, and an opportunity to review and comment on the proposed standards, and it conducts audits related to such standards.

The Board has nine members, appointed equally by the Mayor, the Council, and the Mayor from judicial nominations. Members serve six-year terms, and vacancies are filled for the remainder of the term. The Board is required to establish procedures for the hearing of grievances, complaints, and all requests for assistance made on behalf of any persons confined under the jurisdiction of the Department, or by any employee of the Department. The Board must issue, at least every three years, a report on the Department's grievance process, incorporating feedback from incarcerated individuals and recommendations for improvement, including for vulnerable populations. This report must be submitted to the Council and posted on the Board's website.

The Board is also, in most instances, obligated to investigate and prepare a report about the death of any individual in custody. The report must include any recommendations about the measures or correctional health services that could be implemented in the future to prevent the circumstances that contributed to the individual's death. The report should be posted on the Board's website, along with any response from the Department of Correction and Correctional Health Services. If appointed, Martha King would serve the remainder of the six-year term that ends on February 1, 2029.
