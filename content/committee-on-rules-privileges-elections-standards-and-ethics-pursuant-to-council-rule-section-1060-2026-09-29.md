Meeting Overview

The Committee on Rules, Privileges, Elections, Standards and Ethics convened on September 29, 2026, under Chair Sandra Ung, with a full complement of members including Speaker Nantasha Williams, Majority Leader Shaun Abreu, Minority Leader David Carr, Deputy Majority Leader Chris Banks, and Council Members Encarnacion, Krishnan, Lee, and Riley.
